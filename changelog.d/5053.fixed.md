It is placed as a block flush against the marker is (the closing document's root foot, or the key a dedented block closes). The same on `-P` and on a write (`.z = 1`), with a nested mapping, an indented block, a sequence and `...`. Blank lines *between* two such blocks are still dropped (#5052), and the other two items of #5053 (a dedented second foot block, CRLF comment lines) are open.
