- **jq: a `reduce` UPDATE that destructures an outer `$x` and ends on `.` is no longer refused where jq answers** (#4901, #4832).
  `. as $x | reduce 1 as $i (.; $x as {a:$a} | .)` and `.a as $x | reduce .a as $i (.; $x as $q | $x as {a:$a} | .)` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` are `[]` in jq 1.7.1 (the `del()` of either deletes the document, `|= 9` replaces it); they refused with `Invalid path expression with result <accumulator>` and `near attempt to access element "a"`. A `reduce` stores its UPDATE's output without a path check and judges the final emission on the accumulator's provenance, so a bind in tail position now leaves the `.` it ends on unchecked, and a chain of destructures of a `$x` that ends in `.` hands the accumulator back whether or not the source moved the register; a destructure of the `$x` that IS the register (`.a as $x | reduce .a as $i (.; $x as {b:$q} | .)`) is carried onto the register the source left. `try path(.a as $x | reduce (.a?, .) as $i (.; $x | . as {a:$a} | .)) catch .` now names the step jq raises on at the second source element (`near attempt to access element "a" of {"b":1}`), where it said `with result {"b":1}` after refusing step 1 eagerly. Over a 20,160-row sweep of `reduce` UPDATEs reading a foreign `$x` (three binds x eight sources x three INITs x thirty-five UPDATE shapes x two write contexts x four documents) against `/usr/bin/jq`: 1,466 rows now match, none newly differs, and no row accepts where jq refuses. Still refused where jq answers (recorded in `limitations.md`): the same destructure behind a `try`/`?`, a comma, `first`, an `if` or a `select`, with a `?//` chain or a computed key, or not ending on `.`; and a foreign `$x` inside a fold nested in a `reduce` UPDATE or in a `foreach` UPDATE or EXTRACT still names the end of the path.
