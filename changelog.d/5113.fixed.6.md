- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` whose literal `then` ran beside a fold in the `else` answers on a step that is not the fork's last when the source is longer than eight steps or unbounded** (#5113). The lookahead that proves no later step runs the fold arm on the stored literal stopped at eight steps and needed a bound on the source. The stored literals are drawn from the literals UPDATE names, so the steps cycle: a step that stores a state an earlier one entered with repeats the steps between, and the lookahead reads a longer source through that cycle. A source whose bound no static shape says (`.[]`, `range(n)`) may have any number of steps left, so it is read when every state of the cycle is read as the last step would be. `[path(. as $x | foreach .[] as $i (.; (if .a then 1 else FE end, 7); $i))?]` on `{"a":{"a":null}}` (with `FE` a `foreach` over `try ($x as {a:$a} | .)`) is jq's `[["a"],["a"]]` where it answered `[]`, as is `|= 9` (`{"a":9}`); so is a ten-element source. A `?//` pattern and a state sequence that does not repeat within eight steps keep the whole-`if` resolve.
