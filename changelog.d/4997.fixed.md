- **yq: a write that changes the kind of a document root keeps the comments around the document** (#4997). `# c` over a comment-only file, `null` or `~`, written `.a = 1`, printed `a: 1`; yq prints `# c` / `a: 1`. The same held for `. = [1]` over a scalar or `. = {"a": 1}` over a mapping: the document's head comment, and its foot when the result is a container, were dropped and are kept now. The head comment of a non-first document and a root with an explicit tag are recorded in the yq limitations.
