- **jq: a `foreach` source that destructures a `$x` bound outside it is checked like `. as {a:$a}`** (#4320, second part).
  `. as $x | del(foreach ($x as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .))` deleted the document where jq raises `Invalid path
  expression`, and `=`/`|=` through it wrote the same way, as did a `$x` that is some other node (`.a as $x |
  del(foreach ($x as {b:$b} | .) ...)`). Outside a fold body every such marker in a source that destructures something is
  read; inside the one body that is known to hold the register, each is certified with the resolver's own marker-identity
  test, so a fold-body `$it` that is a different node stays by value. Over 75,339 sweep rows of every fold operand
  binding a variable, wrong accepts go from 534 to 343 and wrong answers from 52 to 32 with no match lost. A `$x` behind
  `first`/`limit`/`?`, one destructured inside another fold's body, and one returned by a call defined outside the source
  are still accepted (#4320).
