- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a pipe of no-ops (`.|.`) over an untracked state hands it on as a bare `.` does** (#5023). `[path(. as $x | foreach (.) as $i (null; (.|.); $x))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[[]]` in jq and `[]` here, and without the `?` `path(...)` exited 5 with `Invalid path expression with result null` where jq prints `[]`. The no-op chain flattened to nothing and the resolver raised #530's terminal refusal on the state, a check of a value that is not the register; a fold's UPDATE stores its output unchecked, so jq answers from the EXTRACT, as it does for a lone `.`. An INIT that is `null`, a number or `[]`, a comma sibling (`7, (.|.)`, which dropped an output), and `try`/`?` around the pipe are answered; `path(1 | .)` and `path(null | (.|.))` keep the terminal's refusal, and so does a pipe off the root (a bind's body after a pattern step). Over 40,000 rows of a differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 1,341 rows that were refused or answered differently now match, 1 row that matched now refuses loudly (the pipe now behaves as a bare `.` does with an INIT of `[.[]?]` on a `null` document), and none is newly accepted where jq refuses.
