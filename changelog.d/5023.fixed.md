- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is a pipe of no-ops (`.|.`) over an untracked state is read as the bare `.` it is** (#5023). `[path(. as $x | foreach (.) as $i (null; (.|.); $x))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[[]]` in jq and `[]` here, and without the `?` `path(...)` exited 5 with `Invalid path expression with result null` where jq prints `[]`. The no-op chain flattened to nothing and the resolver raised #530's terminal refusal on the state, a check of a value that is not the register; a fold's UPDATE stores its output unchecked, so jq answers from the EXTRACT, as it does for a lone `.`. The fold now reads the UPDATE's no-op pipes as the `.` they are, alone, as a comma item (`7, (.|.)`, which dropped an output), under `try`/`?`, as the left of a `//` and in `first(..)`, over an INIT of `null`, a number or `[]`. A no-op pipe anywhere else (`path(null | (.|.))`, a bind's body after a pattern step) keeps the refusal it had: handling it in the shared pipe resolver answered shapes jq refuses. Over 30,000 rows of a fold-focused differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 2,328 rows that were refused or answered differently now match, none that matched differ. Over 40,000 rows of a grid of 32 shapes that put a no-op pipe in a fold, a bind, a `//`, a `try` or a comma: 803 now match, none that matched differ, none is newly accepted where jq refuses, and 23 that answered `"C"` wrongly now refuse loudly (the bare `.` with a computed array INIT already did, #5024).
