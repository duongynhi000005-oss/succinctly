- **yq: a variable bound from an absent key has no candidates** (#2650). `.zz as $x | $x` on `a: null` printed `null` and `[.zz as $x | $x]` printed `[null]`; both now print nothing and `[]` as yq does, and `.c | (.a as $x | $x) | path` no longer answers `["c","a"]` for a position yq never creates. The `as` source reads under the same read-only absent-key rule as a binary-operator operand (#2470), on the lazy, eager, owned-identity and path-position routes; the body keeps its own scope, so `.zz as $x | .zz = 3` still creates `zz`. An index on a mapping (`.[0] as $x`) is unchanged.
