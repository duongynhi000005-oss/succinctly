- **jq: a field navigation ahead of a bare nested `foreach` that destructures the register `$x` no longer makes a refusal `?` cannot catch** (#5107). With `.b as $x | foreach . as $i (.; .b | foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); null)` on `{"a":null,"b":{"a":1}}`, the `.b` stage moves jq's register to `["b"]`, where `$x` is bound, the bind passes and steps it to `["b","a"]`, and EXTRACT's `null` is refused there (`Invalid path expression with result null`, catchable: `[path(..)?]` is `[]`). The emission was judged at `["b"]` and refused as `with result {"a":1}`, a guess that `?` did not catch (exit 5 for `[path(..)?]`, where jq prints `[]`). The bare nested `foreach` has been restated at the register its bind moved to since #4957 and behind a literal stage since #5110; behind field navigations the restatement now starts at the child they reach, where every `$x` the UPDATE reads must equal that child (an absent child or a marker bound elsewhere keeps the reading it had). Over 18,000 grid rows (document x bind site x navigation x nested `foreach` x wrapper x source x INIT x EXTRACT x context) against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 26 rows that were refused or answered wrongly now match, none that matched differ.
