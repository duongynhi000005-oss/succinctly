- **jq: an `isempty`/`any`/`all` as the UPDATE of a fold nested in another fold's UPDATE keeps the register** (#4699, #4661).
  `[path(foreach .a? as $k (0; reduce (1,2) as $j (1; isempty(reduce (1,2) as $i (1; try .a))); $k))?]` on `null` is `[["a"]]` in jq and `[]` here (the refusal was swallowed by the `?` and the stream element dropped; without `?` it raised `Invalid path expression with result null`), as did the `foreach` spelling `[path(foreach .a? as $k (0; foreach (1,2) as $j (1; isempty(1); .); $k))?]` (jq: `[["a"],["a"]]`), with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. A nested `foreach`/`reduce` whose UPDATE is a verdict builtin over operands that leave the register now leaves it too, as a neutral UPDATE does. A nested EXTRACT that reads the enclosing loop variable after a caught navigation (`... isempty(reduce ... try .a); $k`) is unchanged: it is the frozen-`$var` withhold (#4499) and is still refused where jq answers.
