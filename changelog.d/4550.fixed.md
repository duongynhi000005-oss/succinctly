- **jq: a `foreach`/`reduce` whose UPDATE is a `try` over a `?//` bind of a container accumulator no longer swallows the retry** (#4550).
  `path(reduce .a? as $k ([]; try (. as [$a] ?// $a | $a)))` on `null` raises `Invalid path expression with result []` in jq and answered `[]` here, and
  `path(foreach .a? as $k ([]; try (. as [$a] ?// $a | $a); $k))` is `["a"]` in jq and was nothing (`{}` and `[1]` accumulators the same). The accumulator is a computed
  container, which jq's `path_intact` refuses at the pattern's first step whatever the register is, and `?//` retries onto the bare alternative. No register was
  lost, so the refusal handed back was catchable as jq's own, but `refusal_is_exact` called it a guess and never retried, so the `try` swallowed it. An outermost
  fold now marks each step whose accumulator the walk places off the register (not INIT `.`, which is the register on the first step), and a refusal on such a step
  is retried as jq's own.
