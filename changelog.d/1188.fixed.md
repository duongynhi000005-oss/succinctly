- **`succinctly yq` reads a flow collection followed by `:` as a mapping key** (#1188). `[a, b]: value` and `{a: 1}: value` are `{"":"value"}` where they were the collection and the value as two separate results, and a sibling after them (`z: 1`) joined neither; `k:` / `  [a, b]: value` no longer loses its value. The same holds under `- `, after `? k` / `:`, behind an `&anchor` or `!tag`, and for `? [1, 2]: value` (`{"":null}`), matching yq in each case.
