- **jq: a composed `isempty`/`any` verdict (`| not`) under a `try`/`?` as a `foreach` UPDATE keeps the register** (#4719, #4706).
  `[path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; try ((isempty(1) | not)); $k))?]` on `[1]` is `[[0]]` in jq and was `[]` here (the refusal was swallowed by the outer `?` and the stream element dropped; without it, `Invalid path expression with result 1`), as were `try ((isempty(recurse) | not))` and `try (any(1; .) | not)`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. `try E` and `E?` add no movement of their own, so a pipe or comma under one is read as it is bare.
