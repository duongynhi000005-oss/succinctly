- **jq: `capture` and `scan` emit `null` for capture groups that did not participate in the match** (#5363). An optional or alternative group omitted from a match was dropped from the object (`capture`) or omitted from the array (`scan`), where jq keeps it as `null`. For example, `"b" | capture("(?<x>a)?(?<y>b)")` emitted `{"y":"b"}` instead of `{"x":null,"y":"b"}`, and `"a" | [scan("(a)(b)?")]` emitted `[["a"]]` instead of `[["a",null]]`. Both builtins now preserve non-participating capture slots as `null`, matching jq's behavior.
