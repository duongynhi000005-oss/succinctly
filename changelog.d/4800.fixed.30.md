- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` with a literal `else` is resolved as that arm behind a `try ... catch` and beside an emitting comma item** (#4800).
  `[path(. as $x | foreach .a as $i (.; try (if .a then foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) else null end) catch 1; .a))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[["a","a"]]` in jq and raised an uncatchable `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of null`; with a `$i` EXTRACT it is `[["a"]]` and was `[]`.
  The same `if` beside a comma item that cannot move the register (`(U, 7)`) answers `[["a"],["a"]]` for `$i`, as jq does, and `path`, `del` and `|=` match jq, for any literal `else` (`null`, `1`, `"s"`) and over the sources `.`, `.a` and `(.,.a)`.
  A literal arm cannot raise, so the handler never runs, and the comma's items are resolved one at a time; a `then` that ran, a computed `else`, and an arm that navigates ahead of its fold keep the whole-`if` resolve.
