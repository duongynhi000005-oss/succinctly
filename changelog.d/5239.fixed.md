- **jq: a `foreach` or `reduce` in a `path`/`del`/`pick`/assignment target is refused beside `line`, `at_offset`, `key` or another cursor builtin** (#5239). Such a program stayed on the shipped resolver after #5223 and answered #5225, #5226 and #5228's shapes wrongly: 12 of those issues' 67 rows were silent wrong accepts (exit 0 and a landed write, `del` and `|=` printing the document unchanged, where jq exits 5 with `Invalid path expression with result 5`), and 19 more refused a program jq answers. They now raise `<builtin> cannot be combined with a foreach or reduce in a path target` (exit 5), so no row is a wrong accept; a dynamic target without a fold keeps working. Recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`.
