- **jq: an `isempty`/`any`/`all` over a `foreach` with a forking EXTRACT, a `recurse` generator or a `?//` bind keeps the register for a fold's EXTRACT** (#4698, #4661).
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; isempty(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; ($i, 5))); $k))` on `null` is `["a","a"]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result null` here, as did `any(recurse; .)` and `isempty(recurse)` as the UPDATE (jq: `["a"]`) and `[path(foreach .a? as $k (0; isempty(. as $q ?// $z | .); $k))?]` (jq: `[["a"],["a"]]`, here `[]`: the refusal was swallowed by the `?` and the stream elements dropped), with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. A forking EXTRACT now states the register UPDATE reached for every branch, a recursion's seed states the register the verdict entered with, and a `?//` bind whose body steps nowhere raises nothing inside a verdict's operand, whose outputs jq throws away. An EXTRACT that navigates after its first output, and a verdict over a fold inside a second fold's UPDATE, are unchanged (still refused where jq answers).
