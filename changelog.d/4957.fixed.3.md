- **jq: a `null`/boolean EXTRACT after a bare caught-bind `foreach` UPDATE is judged against the register the bind moved** (#4957). `try path(. as $x | foreach (.,.a) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); true)) catch "C"` on `{"a":null}` is `"C"` in jq and exited 5 here with `Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`, a refusal no `?` or `try` catches; `path(.. foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; FE; null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]`, and `(.. ) |= 5` and `del` follow it. Where the inner bind passes, jq's register moves onto the bound child, the `foreach` emits its state with the register there, and the EXTRACT's `null`/`true`/`false` is that node (`jv_identical` holds those equal by kind) or a raise. The fold now states that register, so the EXTRACT's emission is the child's path or jq's own error. Only a `foreach` that is the whole UPDATE (or sits in a `try`/`?`) with a one-bind source that has no `catch .` handler is read, under an EXTRACT that cannot move the register; a handler, a navigating EXTRACT and the wrapped (`if`/pipe) UPDATEs keep the refusal they had.
