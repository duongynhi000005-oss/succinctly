- **jq: a `first`/`limit` of a `?//` chain as a `foreach` source inside another `foreach`'s UPDATE prints its path once per step, not twice, on a `null` document** (#4775, #4669).
  `path(. as $x | {a:{b:1}} | (foreach (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (first(. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .)) as $k (.; .; .); .)) | $x)` on `null` printed `[] [] [] []` where jq prints `[] []`. The head is a freshly built container, no node of jq's register, so the chain's destructuring alternative raises at its first step and jq retries onto the bare one, which is the only alternative that emits; the by-value drive of the whole chain emitted the destructured alternative's output as well. The chain is now read as its bare last alternative there. Over 14,000 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (`path`, `del`, `|=`, 7 documents, `first`/`limit`/`nth`/`try`/`?` wrappers, `foreach`/`reduce` hosts): 85 rows that differed now match, none that matched differ, none that refused now accepts.
