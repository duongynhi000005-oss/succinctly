- **jq: a `foreach`/`reduce` under a `?//` bind no longer answers wrongly or refuses where jq answers** (#4488).
  `path(. as $x | . | (try (foreach . as [$a] ?// $a ({"a":5}; .a; .)) catch 7) | $x)` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq and was `[]` here:
  the fold's UPDATE refused its navigation of the literal INIT (jq's own path error, which sends the chain to its next alternative
  with the state `null`), and the `try` around the fold swallowed the refusal into its handler; `del`/`=`/`|=` raised instead of writing.
  A fold's `?//` chain now retries on that refusal (top level only). And a chain that *is* a fold's UPDATE falls back to its bare `$x`
  with the register where the step left it, which it now states, as does a `reduce` UPDATE (it backtracks its pattern's steps), so
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; (. as [$a] ?// $a | 0); $k))` and `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; reduce . as [$a] ?// $a (0; .); $k))`
  on `null` are `["a"]` rather than `Invalid path expression with result null`. Over the 29 new `?//` fold operands in
  `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (31,695 `--stage-only` rows) against `main`: wrong accepts 3 to 0, wrong refusals 835 to 475,
  matches 30,817 to 31,184, no regression; over all 147 `reduce`/`foreach` operands holding a `?//` (151,893 rows) wrong refusals
  go 7,327 to 6,905, wrong accepts 83 to 80, no regression. `yq` mode is unchanged.
