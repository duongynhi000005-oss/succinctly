- **jq: a fold whose `UPDATE` is `isempty` over a `?//` bind keeps the register on the body's frame** (#4354). The fold body withheld the register whenever its `UPDATE` held a destructuring `as`, because a `?//` can fan out into siblings that see the register differently. An `isempty(g)` hands every answer to its consumer itself, each at the register the arm states, and its identity element at the frame's register, so a `?//` inside its operand is not such a sibling; with the register withheld the identity had none to state and the emission was refused. `path(foreach .[]? as $k (.; isempty(. as $q ?// $z | if $q then true else empty end) or (.a)?; .))` on `[true]` is now jq's `[0]` (it exited 5 with `Invalid path expression with result true`), and so are `... or true` (`[0]`, `[0]`) and `del(foreach .[]? as $k (.; isempty(...) or (.a)?; .b?))` (`[true]`). An operand with any other fan-out (a comma, a fold, a call) or one that reads a frozen loop variable still withholds the register, as before, and so does `any`/`all`, whose `cond` runs a live stage against that register.
