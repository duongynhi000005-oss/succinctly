- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` whose literal `then` ran beside a fold in the `else` answers on a step that is not the fork's last when every later step stores a literal too** (#5113). The step stores the literal as the state, and the next step runs UPDATE on it: when that run raises on the literal and a literal handler answers, or takes a literal `else`, no step runs the fold on a stored literal the resolve cannot place, so the `then` is read as that arm. `[path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; try (if .a then 1 else FE end) catch 1; $i))?]` on `{"a":{"a":null}}` (with `FE` a `foreach` over `try ($x as {a:$a} | .)`) is jq's `[["a"],[]]` where it answered `[]`. The lookahead runs at most eight steps ahead, for a bounded source, a single pattern and an UPDATE that is the `if` itself behind a handler, `?` or a no-op stage; any later step that would run the fold keeps the whole-`if` resolve. Over 160,000 rows of `scripts/jq-foreach-update-if-arm-sweep.py` against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build, no row that matched jq was lost and no refusal turned into a wrong answer.
