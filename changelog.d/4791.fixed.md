- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure `foreach` whose `$x` is the whole document (`. as $x`) in a `reduce` UPDATE is read as the register, not as a foreign node** (#4791, #4778).
  With `P = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)` on `{"a":{"b":1}}`, `path(. as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; select(.a) | P))` is `[]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result {"a":{"b":1}}` here, and `path(. as $x | reduce .. as $i (.; (. | P)))` raises `result null` in jq and said `result {"a":{"b":1}}`.
  `$x` bound at the root is the register where nothing moved it, and on the step of a moving source (`..`, `.[]`, `(.,.a)`) whose element is the root: its pattern step passes and the `foreach` emits. On the other steps of a moving source it is foreign and the step leaves `null`, as before. The UPDATE shapes around the `foreach` (`select(C) | FE`, `FE // 5`, `if C then FE else . end`) now run on the register at their first step, so the document-bound `$x` is not mistaken for a node that may have been bound elsewhere. A `null` document keeps refusing under `del` and `|=` as jq does.
