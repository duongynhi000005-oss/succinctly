- **jq: a `try`/`?` around `first(., X)` alone, ahead of a destructure of `.` in a moved `reduce` body's source, no longer hides the raise** (#4676).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (try first(., {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq and was `null` here (`path(...)` printed `[[]]`, `=`/`|=` replaced the document), because #4667's read of `first(., X)`/`limit(1; ., X)`/`nth(0; ., X)` as `.` was withheld for any `try`/`?` anywhere in the source. Only a `try`/`?` whose body holds the destructure can catch its raise, so the read is now withheld for that case alone (`try (first(., X) | . as {a:$a} | .)` and `(...)?` are still answered, as in jq).
