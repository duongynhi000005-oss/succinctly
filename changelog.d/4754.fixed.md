- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure in a `foreach` source, through a `select(.)` stage, a `try`/`?`, an `// empty`/`// LITERAL`, an `if . then ... else . end` or a `label`, as a `reduce` UPDATE, is refused as jq refuses it, and the refusal is catchable by an outer `?`** (#4754, #4730).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; select(.) | foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` raises `Invalid path expression with result null` in jq and printed `[]` here (`del(...)` deleted the document, `|=` replaced it); with `// 5` after the `foreach` the state is `5` and jq says `result 5`. A `try` in the source may nest and end `// empty`. The refusal is jq's own end-of-path one, as for #4730's bare `foreach`, so `[path(...)?]` is `[]`. A comma beside the `foreach` and a fallback to `.` are unchanged.
