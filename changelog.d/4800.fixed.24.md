- **jq: an `if` around the caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` in a `foreach` UPDATE is read on a condition other than `.`, behind a `try`/`?` and with a `.` or `select(true)` stage after it** (#4800).
  `[path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; if .a then foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) else . end; $i))?]` on `{"a":true}` is `[]` in jq and was refused naming the state, and, with `FE` that `foreach`, `[path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (if . then (FE, empty) else . end) | .; .a))?]` on `{"a":null}` (jq `[]`) was refused until it could not be: read like the bare `if`, the `.a` EXTRACT's index error escaped the `?`. jq runs an `if`'s condition in a subexp, so a condition that is a pure function of `.` moves no register; the step whose state takes the `else` branch is now read as that branch alone for such a condition and behind a handler-less `try`/`?` or a pipe whose later stages move nothing, and `select(true)` is read as `.` after the `if`. This lifts the three refusals #5010 kept on the comma `if` (`if .a then (FE, empty) else . end`, `(if . then (FE, empty) else . end) | .`, `try (if ...)`/`(if ...)?`) in a `foreach` UPDATE, and fixes `| select(true)` and the bare `if .a then FE else . end`; elsewhere (a `foreach` EXTRACT, a `reduce` UPDATE, behind a head stage, `first`/`limit`/`catch`, a tail that computes) the comma `if` keeps the answer it had. Against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1: on the 16,200-row grid of #5019 (18 UPDATE spellings x 4 sources x 5 EXTRACTs x 5 write contexts x 9 documents) 1,277 rows fixed (every row that differed), none lost, none newly accepted wrongly; on a 70,560-row grid of `if` UPDATEs on pure and other conditions (28 spellings x 6 sources x 7 EXTRACTs x 5 write contexts x 12 documents) 1,256 fixed, none lost, no new wrong accept; the 31,500-row control grid is unchanged (0 fixed, 0 lost).
