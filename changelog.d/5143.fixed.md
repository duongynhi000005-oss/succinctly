- **jq: a `reduce` whose source moves the register no longer accepts a discarded `reverse`, `transpose`, `index`, `any(gen; cond)`, `$z.b` or one-branch `if`** (#5143). `(reduce .. as $i (.; (reverse), .)) |= 9` on `[[1],[2]]` raises `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element ..` in jq and wrote `9` (so did `transpose`, `index(1)`, `(any(.[]; .)), .` and `(. as $z | $z.b), .` on `{"a":{"b":1},"b":2}`, and `if . then (map(.)), . else . end`): after the source moves jq's register onto its element, any path-checked index of the accumulator raises, even when the UPDATE discards its output. #5141 read the shapes that index on every accumulator; these index only a container with something to index (`reverse` a non-empty one, `transpose` one holding a non-empty element, `index`/`rindex`/`indices` all but a string), through a variable bound to the accumulator or a generator, or in the branch an `if` takes. They are now read per step, from the accumulator the step holds: an `if` whose condition is effect-free is evaluated on it (a variable bound to it included) and each output runs its branch, so an empty container, a string or an untaken branch still answers as jq does, and a variable bound to the accumulator feeds a builtin, a rebinding or a generator as `.` does.
