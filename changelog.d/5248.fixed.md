- **jq: `..` followed directly by a bracket suffix is read as a postfix on the `..` term** (#5248). `[[1]] | ..[0]?` and `[..[]?]` were parse errors (`unexpected character '['`) where jq 1.7.1 answers `[1]`, `1` and `[[1],1]`, and the same for `..["a"]?`, `..[1:]?`, `.. [0]?` and `.. .a?`; the workarounds were `.. | .[0]?` and `(..)[0]?`. The suffix keeps its path register, so `path(..[0]?)`, `del(..[0]?)`, `(..[0]?) |= 9` and `= 9` answer as jq does. An ad hoc grid of 1,225 rows (7 documents x 25 suffix spellings x read, `path`, `try path`, `del`, `|=`, `=`) against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 differed on 1,008 before and on none now; `tests/jq_cli/i5248.rs` pins 119 of them, plus `..` beside `as`, `//`, `reduce`, `first`, `limit`, `label` and a `def`. yq mode is unchanged: yq's lexer rejects `..[0]?` (`Error: bad expression`), so `succinctly yq` still does.
