- **yq: `line` and `column` of a node with an `&anchor` or `!tag` are the property's position** (#2712).
  `b: &anc [1,2]` answered column 9, the `[`, where yq v4.53.3 answers 4, the `&`; the same held for
  every tagged or anchored scalar (`a: !!str 5` was column 10, yq 4), and for an anchored or tagged
  mapping or sequence whose value starts on the next line the *line* was wrong too (`e: &z` / `  k: 1`
  was line 6, yq 5). The position is the node's first property token whichever order the anchor and
  tag come in (`&x !!str 5` and `!!str &y 5`), in block, flow, sequence-item and explicit-key
  positions; a node with no property, an alias, and `at_offset`/`yq-locate` are unchanged. Still
  different from yq, as before: the root of a first document that opens with `---` or a comment
  (yq says 1:1; #4363), and columns after multi-byte text (bytes here, characters in yq; #4364).
