- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer beside an `empty` spelled out** (#4800).
  `path(foreach (1,2) as $i (.; (. as {a:$q} | .), select(false); null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` twice in jq (`select(false)` stores nothing, so the state is the bind's output alone); it was refused naming `{"a":null}`, and `[path(...)?]` answered `[]` where jq answers `[["a"],["a"]]`. `select(false)`, `select(null)`, `first(empty)`, `first(select(false))`, `limit(0; ..)` and `limit(N; empty)` beside the bind now match over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A sibling that is no bind (`(B), 7`) and `select(1)` keep their old reading.
