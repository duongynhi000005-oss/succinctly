- **yq: block indentation after stacked compact sequence items follows yq's round-up rule** (#1881). go-yaml opens a block child under a mapping value at the smallest multiple of `-I` above the current indent, and a compact `- ` adds a literal 2 columns, so with two stacked `- - b:` levels over `c:` the column of `c` is 6, 6, 8, 5, 6 at `-I=2..6` (not monotonic), and with three stacked levels `-I=4` and `-I=5` do not follow the pattern either. succinctly stepped from a pre-compact base instead and disagreed at `-I=3` (two levels) and `-I=4`/`-I=5` (three levels). One rounding step (`deeper_yaml_indent`) is now shared by the cursor writer, the DOM writer and the owned-value streamer; output outside a compact chain is unchanged (the indent is already a multiple of `-I` there). The "stacked compact items" entry in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md` is removed as no longer a divergence.
