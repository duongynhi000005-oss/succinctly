- **jq: a `//` over a bind of an outer `$x` in a `reduce` or `foreach` UPDATE is read when a bind in its right operand takes its source from a navigation of an outer `$x`** (#5175). A bind whose source is `$x.a` or `$x.a.b` (`// ($x.a as $q | $q)`) kept the resolver's refusal, which a `try`/`?` around the `//` swallowed, so `path`/`del`/`|=` answered (or wrote) where jq raises at the emission of the left operand's truthy output: `path(. as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (false; try (($x as {a:$a} | 5) // ($x.a as $q | $q))))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` and `(. as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (null; try (($x as {a:$a} | 5) // ($x.a as $q | $q)))) |= 9` on `null` are `Invalid path expression with result 5` (exit 5) in jq, and answered nothing and `9` here. jq runs a bind's source in a subexpression that tracks no path, so such a source moves no register, as `.a` and a bare `$x` do. Over every third row of the sweep grid with such an operand (`scripts/jq-fold-update-alt-bind-sweep.py`, against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build) 856 of 35,280 `reduce` and 1,637 of 35,280 two-argument `foreach` wrong accepts now match jq, 263 and 255 wrong refusals now answer as jq does, and no row that matched before differs. Still open (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`): a `?//` chain behind the `//` in a `reduce`, a `reduce` whose final accumulator is the register's node, and an operand with a `def` or a call.
