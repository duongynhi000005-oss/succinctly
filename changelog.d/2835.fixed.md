- **yq: a tag or anchor alone on the line under a bare `-` or `key:` is kept, and the sibling key after it is no longer swallowed** (#2835).
  `printf -- '-\n  !!str\n- 2\n' | yq .` is `- !!str` / `- 2` and was a bare `-` here (`.[0] | tag` answered `!!null`, `.[0] | anchor` empty); `a:\n  !!str\nb: 1` is `a: !!str` / `b: 1` and lost `b` altogether (`.a` read `b`, `keys` listed one key). The properties name an empty node, as they do on the dash's own line. A comment on that bare `-` (`- # c` / `  !!str` / `- 2`) now goes forward as the next node's head (`- !!str` / `# c` / `- 2`), where it was dropped with the tag; it does not become the owner key's foot the way an absent item's does (`k:` / `  - # c` / `    !!str` / `z: 1` heads `z`). Pinned against yq v4.53.3 for a tag, an anchor, both, nested, at end of input and with a comment on the dash; the comment after the properties (`!!str # d`) still sits on the item where yq moves it onto the next one.
