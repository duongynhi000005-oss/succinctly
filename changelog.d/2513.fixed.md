- **yq: a write no longer re-declares the anchors of a merge source** (#2513). `.item.b = 3` over `default: &d` / `  a: &y 1` / `item:` / `  <<: *d` / `  b: 2` printed a second `a: &y 1` under `item` (the merge is expanded on the write path), so every later `*y` resolved to the copy instead of the declaration; the merged-in fields now carry no `&anchor` or tag of their own, as an alias expansion's do (#2500). An inline source (`<<: {a: &y 1}`), written nowhere else, keeps its declaration, and an alias inside the merged fields is kept. The expansion itself, where yq keeps `<<: *d`, stays recorded in the yq limitations.
