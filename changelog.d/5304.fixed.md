- **jq: `builtins` lists jq 1.7.1's builtins, all 218 of them and no other** (#5304). It listed 187: it lacked 61 callable jq builtins (`match/2`, `capture/1`, `sub/3`, `JOIN/4`, `format/1`, `input_filename/0`, the libm family from `cbrt/0` to `yn/2`, ...) and listed names jq 1.7.1 does not define (`trim/0`, `ltrim/0`, `rtrim/0`, `leaf_paths/0`, `skip/2`, `toboolean/0`, `reduce/3`, `foreach/3`, `key/0`, `parent/0`, the yq metadata getters, ...). `builtins` is now byte-identical to jq's, in jq's order, so `builtins | index("trim/0")` and `builtins | length` give jq's answers. A name that is only an extension here is still callable; it is just not introspected. The list is the roster `tests/data/jq-builtin-names.txt` that the compile-time resolver already pins against the oracle.
