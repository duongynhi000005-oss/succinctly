- **jq: a fold source that destructures a `$x` bound elsewhere than the register no longer answers (and writes) where jq raises** (#4832, #4588).
  `.a as $x | reduce ($x as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .)` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` printed `[]`, its `del()` printed `null` (the document deleted) and `|= 1` replaced the document, where jq 1.7.1 raises `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of {"b":1}`; the same held behind a comma, a `1 |` stage, a `select(false)`, a `first` or a `label`, with a computed key (`{("a"):$a}`), an array pattern or a bind in front, and for a `foreach` source with a stage ahead of the destructure. The pattern's tracked step raises in jq whether or not the `reduce` backtracks its source; a fold source outside every fold body that destructures such a `$x` is now routed to the resolver, which decides what the stages around it skip. Over a 14,976-row grid of foreign-`$x` navigations and destructures in fold sources, UPDATEs and EXTRACTs against `/usr/bin/jq`: 162 wrong accepts now match and 180 message-only rows with them, none newly differ. Still by value, as before (recorded in `limitations.md`): a fold entered after a stage or an INIT that navigated, a `$x` bound outside the `path()`/`del()` expression, a destructure of `.` ahead of the `$x` one, and a source with an `if`/`try`/`and`/`or`/`//` around it.
