- **jq: the identity answer of `any`/`all`/`isempty` is at the register the call entered with on an untracked entry** (#4341). The `true`/`false` that fires once a generator ran to its end (after a swallowed `?//` answer, or when it emitted nothing) stated no register when the call sat on a computed value, so an `and`/`or` around it, or a `foreach` extract navigating it, refused where jq answers: `del(. as $x | 1 | (isempty(empty) or true) | $x.a?)` on `{"a":1}` exited 5 where jq prints `{}`, and so did `any(empty; .) or true`, `all(empty; .) and false` and `any(g ?// ..; .) or true` inside an outer destructuring `?//`. They now carry the frame's register, the one jq's retry backtracked to (#4114 did this for `isempty` after a swallowed answer only). A `foreach` whose UPDATE reaches the identity through an `or` with a right operand that navigates still refuses (#4130).
