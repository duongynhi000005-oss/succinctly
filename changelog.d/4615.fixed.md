- **jq: a `foreach` whose source is a destructuring `?//` chain behind a `def` call with arguments or a nested `foreach` now answers on an untracked entry, as jq does** (#4615).
  `def f(g): g; path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach f((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .)) | $x)` on `[[1],[1]]` is `[]` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result [[1],[1]]` here,
  and so was the chain as the source of a nested `foreach`. The gate that states the register an untracked `foreach` leaves ([#4489](https://github.com/rust-works/succinctly/issues/4489)'s)
  read neither; it now reads the bound body of a call (a closure argument naming the chain, or a `def` written in the source), the argument shared into it, and a nested `foreach`
  whose INIT, UPDATE and EXTRACT cannot move the register and whose loop pattern is a bare variable. A body, UPDATE or EXTRACT that navigates after the bind, and a chain with no bare alternative,
  stay refused as in jq. A `foreach` inside another fold's body is still refused. Same sweep as #4485 (0 regressions, no lost match).
