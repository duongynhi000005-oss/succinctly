- **jq: `isempty(..)` over a `?//` chain, as a `foreach` source in `path()`, now prints the paths jq prints** (#4819, #4669).
  `path(foreach (isempty(. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) | select(.|not)) as $k (.; .; .))` on `{"a":1}` printed `[]` twice where jq prints it once, with or without a comma stage in front of the chain (`(., 1) | CHAIN`), under a `try`/`?`, or with more stages after the `isempty`. jq defines `isempty` with a `label`/`break` that the chain's `?//` catches and retries, so the retry's emission is refused at the end of `path()`; the by-value drive counted both. These sources now go to the resolver's `isempty` arm, as the `first`/`limit`/`nth` ones do (#4669). The same source inside another fold's body keeps its previous count (recorded in `limitations.md`); value mode is unchanged.
