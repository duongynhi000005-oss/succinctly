- **jq: a destructure of `.` on the left of a `//`, as a `reduce` source in a moved `reduce` body, now raises as jq does** (#4674).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (first(., {a:1}) | (. as {a:$a} | .) // 3) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq and was `null` here (`path(...)` printed `[[]]`, `=`/`|=` replaced the document); so did `(. as {a:$a} | .) // 3 | .`, `. | (. as {a:$a} | .) // 3` and `first(.) | (. as {a:$a} | .) // 3`. The left of a `//` always runs, so the register walk now reads a destructure it begins with as the source's own, in a moved body only and not under a `try`/`?` that holds a destructure (the catch answers, as in jq). A navigating stage after `first(.)` is #4685.
