- **`succinctly yq` reads a block scalar header on its own line below a key that carries properties** (#4947). `a: &x` / `  |` / `  text` / `b: 1` parsed to `{"a":"text\n","text":"b"}` and lost `b`; it now gives yq's `{"a":"text\n","b":1}`, with a tag, an anchor, both, or a `>` header alike. The same shape under a bare key, under a dash, or with the header on the key's own line was already right.
