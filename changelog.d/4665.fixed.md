  A closure-taking `def` that only calls its parameter, handed a comma, binds the argument's leaves as the comma does, and a call whose body hands the register on next to a fresh value is met by the stage after it, as a literal comma is; a source whose argument is all the register, or whose `first(f)` stops before the fresh item, is still answered.
