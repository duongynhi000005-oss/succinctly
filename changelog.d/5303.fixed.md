- **jq: `strptime` reads `%s`** (#5303). `"1425599621" | strptime("%s") | mktime` was `date "1425599621" does not match format "%s"`, where jq 1.7.1 answers `1425599621` (under `TZ=UTC`). The digits (after optional white space and one sign) are seconds since the epoch, expanded through the local zone as `localtime_r` does, so `strptime` there is `[2015,2,5,23,53,41,4,63]` in UTC and `[2015,2,6,10,53,41,5,64]` in `Australia/Sydney`; it replaces the date and time read before it (`"2015 1425599621" | strptime("%Y %s")` is the instant's own year). The second item of #5303, a format that leaves fields unset (`"10:15" | strptime("%H:%M")` is `[1970,0,1,10,15,0,4,0]` here, `[1900,0,0,10,15,0,8,367]` on glibc per the issue and `[1900,0,0,10,15,0,6,-1]` on the macOS pin), depends on the platform's libc and is tracked on its own.
