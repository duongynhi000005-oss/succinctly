- **yq: a scalar the source wrote as nothing, `~` or `True` keeps its text on the DOM route** (#2875). `-P '.a'` on `a:` printed `null` where yq prints an empty line (and `~` for `a: ~`, `0x1F` for `a: 0x1F`): the pass that clears a bare result's styling also cleared its spelling. A comment-only document (`# c`) or a bare `---` under `-P` printed a `null` line after its header, and `.c = .a` wrote `c: null` for an empty `a:`. Each now matches yq byte for byte, and a plain `=` from a static read (`.c = .a`, `.c.d = .a.x`, `.[2] = .[0]`) copies the spelling of the node it reads. Not part of this change: a construction (`[.a]`, `{"x": .a}`), `map(.)` over a mapping, and the rest of the node yq copies (quoting, flow style, a trailing comment) still print the plain value.
