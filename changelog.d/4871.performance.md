- **yq: numbering the documents of a stream, and streaming a document whose aliases follow their anchors, no longer cost the square of the document** (#4871). `document_index` found a document's place by counting the documents before it, so `select(document_index == 5)` over a stream of 64,000 two-key documents took 12.4 s; it now binary-searches a list of document positions built once on first use (0.05 s). And a far hop forward in the YAML position cursors (`EndPositions`/`AdvancePositions::get_sequential`: an alias read after its anchor, then the next alias) scanned the interval bitmap word by word across the whole gap; past four select samples' worth of ones it now uses the sampled select the backward jumps already used. `yq -o=json .` over 256,000 anchors followed by 256,000 aliases takes 0.31 s, from 8.9 s. Output is byte-identical (M4 Pro, release). Ordinary documents keep the short scan: `users`, `nested`, `comprehensive`, `sequences` and a 128,000-member mapping retire within about 1% of the previous instruction count on `.`, `.[]`, a tag walk, `keys` and `to_entries` (the +0.4% on `.` is the same on every corpus with no query-specific cause: layout).
