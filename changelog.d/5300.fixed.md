- **jq: string repetition by a negative fraction or `nan` is `null`** (#5300). `"abc" * -0.5` and `"abc" * nan` gave `""`, where jq 1.7.1 gives `null`: the count was truncated before its sign was tested, so `-0.5` became `0`, and `nan` cast to `0` too. A count in `[0, 1)` is still `""` and `-0.0` still is; `succinctly yq` refuses a fractional count as before.
