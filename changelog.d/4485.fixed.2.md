- **jq: a `foreach` source `?//` chain behind a nested `foreach` that hands its loop variable on, behind two partial-register stages, or behind a `def` parameter bound to a literal now answers as jq does** (#4485).
  On a `null` document `path(foreach (foreach (., 1) as $j (.; .; $j) | . as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .))` is jq's `["a"]` `[]` and was `[]` `[]`;
  `path(foreach (foreach (.,1) as $j (.; .; .) | (.,1) | . as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .))` is `["a"]` `[]` `["a"]` `[]` and was `[]` four times;
  `path(foreach (def f($x): . as {a:$a} ?// $a | $x; f(2)) as $k (.; .; .))` is `["a"]` and was `[]`, so a `|=` through any of them wrote at the root.
  A nested `foreach` whose UPDATE or EXTRACT is its single loop variable is read as handing the register on, as `.` is, and an EXTRACT that is a comma of it (or `.`) and a value that moves nothing (`., 1`) as a stage that is the register on some outputs. The two-stage row was
  not a limit of the walk: what a `foreach` emits was resolved at the fold-body marks of its own UPDATE/EXTRACT, so any stage after a nested
  `foreach` was refused with `Invalid path expression with result 1` (one partial stage after one reproduces on `main`); it now emits at the marks
  it was entered with. A parameter bound to a literal is substituted as a shared argument that `cannot_move_register` did not read, and now does.
  Over the 24 operands added with this change (`--stage-only`, 49,947 rows, jq 1.7.1, vs a `main` build): `MATCH` 44,353 -> 44,850, `REFUSE_WRONG` 4,961 -> 4,497,
  `ACCEPT_WRONG` 494 -> 484, `DIFF` 139 -> 116, 0 regressions; a 50,027-row seeded sample of every operand: `MATCH` 47,922 -> 47,938, `ACCEPT_WRONG` (36) and `DIFF` (106) unchanged; a 60,027-row whole-grid sample: `MATCH` 58,069 -> 58,089, `ACCEPT_WRONG` (14) and `DIFF` (60) unchanged.
