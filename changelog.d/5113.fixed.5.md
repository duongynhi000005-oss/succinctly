- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` whose literal `then` ran beside a fold in the `else` answers on a step that is not the fork's last when the UPDATE is a comma with a literal sibling** (#5113). A comma stores its last emission, so the literal the `if` emits is not the state the next step enters with: `(if .a then 1 else FE end, 7)` stores `7`. The lookahead now starts from the sibling's literal and steps through a comma of literals and such `if`s, and a later step whose `if` condition raises with no handler ends the `foreach` (jq's own error), which also lets it be reached past the eight-step cap. `[path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (if .a then 1 else FE end, 7); $i))?]` on `{"a":{"a":null}}` (with `FE` a `foreach` over `try ($x as {a:$a} | .)`) is jq's `[["a"],["a"]]` where it answered `[]`; so are the literal-sibling-first form, a handler `try` around the `if`, and `null`/`true` literals. A sibling that is not a literal, a `?//` pattern and a step the lookahead cannot place keep the whole-`if` resolve.
