- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure in a `foreach` source behind a `.` stage, a `label` or a nested pipe, as a `reduce` or `foreach` UPDATE, is refused as jq refuses it** (#4732, #4724).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; . | foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` raises `Invalid path expression with result null` in jq and printed `[]` here (`del(...)` deleted the document, `|=` replaced it); the same behind `label $l |` or inside `(. | foreach ...) | .`. Under a `foreach` UPDATE the empty source leaves the step emitting nothing, where `. | foreach ...` printed `[]` twice. A comma beside the `foreach` is unchanged.
