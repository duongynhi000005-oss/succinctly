- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer beside an `empty`** (#4800).
  `path(foreach (1) as $i (.; (. as {a:$q} | .), empty; null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq (an `empty` stores nothing, so the state is the bind's output alone and the next step meets the register again); it was refused naming `{"a":null}`, and `[path(...)?]` answered `[]` where jq answers `[["a"]]`. The `empty` ahead of, behind or between binds, and as the tail of a pipe the bind feeds (`(B | empty), B`), now matches over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A sibling that is no bind (`(B), 7`), which stores a node of its own, and an `empty` spelled `select(false)` keep their old reading.
