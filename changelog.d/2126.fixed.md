- **yq: `del()` turns a `null` behind a bracket key into the container the key implies** (#2126).
  `echo null | yq -o=json 'del(.["k0"])'` is `{}` in yq (and `del(.[(0,1)])` is `[]`, `{"a":null} | del(.a["k0"])` is
  `{"a":{}}`), where the dotted `del(.k0)` leaves the `null`; `succinctly yq` printed `null` for all of them. A `del()`
  argument in yq mode keeps a string bracket key apart from the `.k0` field step, and `del()` vivifies the first `null` a literal
  bracket key indexes. The read-side twin (`(.["k0"]) as $x | .` also prints `{}` in yq) is recorded in
  `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
