- **jq: a `foreach` source that is a `?//` chain with a bare `$var` alternative is placed on a `null` register** (#4417).
  `(foreach (. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .)) |= 9` on `null` wrote `9` where jq writes `{"a":9}`, and
  `path(foreach (. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .))` answered `[]` where jq answers `["a"]`: the first alternative's
  walk cannot fail on `null`, so jq moves the register onto the matched member, which the by-value drive never did. Such a
  source is now resolved when the fold's register is a trackable `null`; every other register is unchanged. Over the 33,891
  `--stage-only` rows of the 39 destructuring-fold sweep operands, DIFF 10 -> 0 and MATCH 33,074 -> 33,084, no regression.
