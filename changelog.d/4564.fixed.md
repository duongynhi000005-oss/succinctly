- **jq: a `foreach` nested in a `foreach` UPDATE or EXTRACT, whose source is a `?//` chain that destructures the register, no longer answers where jq raises** (#4564).
  `path(foreach (1,2) as $k (.; .; (foreach ((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// [$a] | .) as $k (.; .; .))))` on `[true]` is `Invalid path expression with result [true]` in jq and was `[]`
  here, and `del(...)` of it deleted the document (`= 9` wrote through). jq emits from inside the outer loop, so the inner fold's emission is checked against the register the chain
  moved; a `reduce` UPDATE backtracks it and still answers `[]`. The same holds for a later bare alternative that is destructured (`. as $x ?// $y | $y as {a:$a} | .`).
  Over the 9 operands that spelled it, in the nine path/write contexts (31,617 rows): `ACCEPT_WRONG` 850 -> 268, 664 rows now match; 150 rows jq answers
  (`CHAIN-FOLD and (.a)?` in an EXTRACT) are refused, the safe direction. A nested `foreach` whose source navigates and then binds with plain `as $v`, and the `try` computed-key rows (#4550), are still accepted.
