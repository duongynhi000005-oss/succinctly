- **yaml: a line deeper than the block, after a block scalar value, is rejected as yq rejects it** (#5078). `a: |` / `   x` / `  y` / `b: 1` read as `{"a":"x\n","y":"b"}` (the `1` gone), `a: |2` / ` x` / `b: 1` as `{"a":"","x":"b"}` and `- |` / `   x` / `  y` / `- z` as `["x\n","y","z"]`: the scalar ended at a line indented less than its content, and a line still deeper than the block that holds the scalar was read as a key or an item of its own. yq stops with `did not find expected key` (`did not find expected '-' indicator` in a sequence), and so does `succinctly yq` now. Blank lines, comment lines and a sibling at the parent's indent are unchanged. A document whose block scalar starts with a `#` line followed by a deeper line (#5073) now errors instead of printing an extra value.
