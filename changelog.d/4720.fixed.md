- **jq: a zero-arity `def` written inside a `foreach` UPDATE around a verdict is read when its scope is more than the one call** (#4720, #4706).
  `[path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; def f: isempty(1); (f | not); $k))?]` on `[1]` is `[[0]]` in jq and was `[]` here (the UPDATE was refused and, under `?`, the stream element dropped), as were `(f , f)`, `def f: recurse; isempty(f)`, `def f: recurse; (isempty(f) | not)` and `def f: 1; (any(f; .) | not)`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. The body is read once and a call in the scope answers as it does; a call as the operand of a verdict builtin is read as the body. A body that navigates (`def f: .a; (f | not)`) and a `def` called from another `def` (`def g: f; (g | not)`) are unchanged and still refused.
