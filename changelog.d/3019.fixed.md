  `.[1:] | .[] | select(.c == 3) | .c | path` on `[{a: 1}, {b: 2}, {c: 3}]` is `[1,"c"]` in yq and answered `["c"]` here, and `.[1:] | .[] | select(.c == 3) | parent` is `[{"b":2},{"c":3}]` in yq and printed nothing; a debug or test build panicked with `an absent position hangs under a real ancestor`.
