- **jq: the caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source is also read behind `.`/`select(true)` stages and a value head such as `not`** (#4800, #4797).
  With `P = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)`, `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; not | P; .))` on `{"a":true}` is `["a"]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result true` here, as did `. | P`, `select(true) | P`, `P | .` and `P | select(true)` on `{"a":null}`. The kind gate now compares the pattern's `null`/boolean landing with the value the `foreach` is handed (`not` of the `null` state is `true`), so `1 | P` and other heads of another kind keep their reading. A `// 5` fallback or an `if` with a `$i` EXTRACT, and a comma beside the `foreach`, are unchanged. Over 77,760 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (the same grid as the first #4800 entry): 81 more rows that differed now match, none that matched differ.
