- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` beside a comma item that emits nothing, in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source, keeps jq's answer** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)), empty; .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq and was refused naming the state (`Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`), and a `?` turned the refusal into `[]`. A comma whose other items are `empty`, `select(false)`, `first(empty)` or `limit(0; ..)` stores exactly what its one emitting item does, so `(FE), empty` and `empty, (FE)` are now read as the bare `foreach` is (`path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`, with a `.`, `$i` or `empty` EXTRACT). A comma that holds a value (`(FE), 7`) keeps its refusal. Over two 20,000-row randomised differential grids against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: about 1,550 rows each that were refused or worded differently now match; the only rows that matched and no longer do (1 and 3) are `if . then (FE, empty) else . end` with a `.a` EXTRACT under `?`, which now shares the bare `if . then FE else . end` form's index error escaping the `?`.
