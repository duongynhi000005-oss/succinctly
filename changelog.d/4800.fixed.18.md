- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` beside a comma item that emits nothing, in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source, keeps jq's answer** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)), empty; .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq and was refused naming the state (`Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`), and a `?` turned the refusal into `[]`. A comma whose other items are `empty`, `select(false)`, `first(empty)` or `limit(0; ..)` stores exactly what its one emitting item does, so `(FE), empty` and `empty, (FE)` are now read as the bare `foreach` is (`path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`, with a `.`, `$i` or `empty` EXTRACT; a `reduce` UPDATE names the `null` state jq names). A comma that holds a value (`(FE), 7`) keeps its refusal, and so does a comma under an `if`. Over a 20,000-row randomised differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 1,409 rows that were refused or worded differently now match, none that matched differ.
