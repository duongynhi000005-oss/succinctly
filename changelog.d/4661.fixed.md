- **jq: an `isempty`/`any`/`all` over a `reduce`, a computed-EXTRACT `foreach` or a non-`null` register keeps the register for a fold's EXTRACT** (#4661, #4499).
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; isempty(reduce (1,2) as $i (1; try .a)); $k))` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result null` here, as did `isempty(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; $i))` (jq: `["a","a"]`), `any(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; .); .)` (jq: `["a"]`) and, on `[1]`, `[path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; isempty(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; .)); $k))?]` (jq: `[[0]]`, here `[]`: the refusal was swallowed by the `?` and the stream element dropped), with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. A `reduce` operand leaves jq's register at the entry, a `foreach` that computes its EXTRACT after a navigating UPDATE states the register UPDATE reached, and an enclosing fold now reads the register a verdict's answer states. A nested fold that reads an outer frozen `$var` is unchanged (still refused where jq answers, #4499).
