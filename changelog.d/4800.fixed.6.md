- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern and hands it back no longer fails with its own state's value** (#4800).
  `path(foreach (1) as $i (.; . as {a:$q} | .; $i))` on `{"a":1}` is `Invalid path expression with result 1` in jq (the EXTRACT's value; the UPDATE is not path-checked, it only stores the register's node as the state) and named `{"a":1}` here; `.a` as EXTRACT now gives jq's index error, and `empty` as EXTRACT, which jq answers with nothing (`del(...)` is the document), no longer fails. The same for `(. as {a:$q} | .) | .`, in `path`, `del`, `|=`, `?` and `try ... catch .`. Over 40,320 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (8 sources x 21 UPDATEs x 10 EXTRACTs x 4 write contexts x 6 documents, against a `main` build): no wrong accept and no row that matched jq stopped matching; 2,121 rows that differed only in the error message now match, 456 rows refused wrongly now answer (an `empty` or computed EXTRACT, including past the first step of a multi-element source) and 86 more that differed in stdout do too. The 1,449 rows left differ only in UPDATEs that are not just binds of `.` (a bind behind a `try` or a `label`, beside a comma, followed by `select(true)` or emitting twice), which keep their old wording or refusal.
