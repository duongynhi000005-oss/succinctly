- **jq: an outer `?` around `path()` of a `reduce` whose UPDATE `foreach` is emptied by a caught foreign-`$x` destructure now catches the refusal, as jq does** (#4730, #4709, #4724).
  `[path(.a as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)))?]` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` is `[]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result null` here, uncatchably. The refusal is certain rather than a resolver guess, so it is now jq's own end-of-path error: a `?`/`try` around the `path()` catches it, one inside still does not.
