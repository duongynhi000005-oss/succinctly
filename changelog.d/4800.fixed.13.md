- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer beside a `limit`, `first` or `nth(0; ..)` of `.` itself** (#4800).
  `[path(foreach (1,2) as $i (.; (. as {a:$q} | .), limit(2; .); null))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[["a"]]` in jq (the sibling emits only `.`, the node the bind already stored, so the state is still the register's own); it was refused naming `{"a":null}` and answered `[]`, and an `empty` EXTRACT raised where jq answers nothing. `limit(N; .)` for a constant nonzero count (`limit(1; .)`, `limit(5/2; .)`, `limit(0.5; .)`, `limit(-1; .)`), `first(.)`, `nth(0; .)`, their nestings and a comma of such (`limit(2; (., .))`) beside the bind now match over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A body that stores a node of its own (`limit(1; .a)`, `limit(1; 7)`), a state-dependent count (`limit(.; .)`) and a count that raises (`limit(1/0; .)`) keep their old reading.
