- **yq: `-j` is now real yq's deprecated `--tojson`, and `--join-output` is removed** (#1731). `succinctly yq
  -j` had been ported from jq's join-output (no newline after each result), a name collision with real yq's
  own `-j`, which forces `-o=json` and prints `Flag --tojson has been deprecated, please use -o=json
  instead` to stderr. `-j` now does that, whichever side of `-o` it is on, and composes with `-I0`, `-r`
  and `-0` as in yq; `--tojson` is accepted too. `--join-output` was never a yq flag (`unknown flag`) and no
  longer exists in `succinctly yq`. `succinctly jq -j` is unchanged.
