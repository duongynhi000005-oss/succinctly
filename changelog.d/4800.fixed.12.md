- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer beside a `limit` whose count is a constant quotient, remainder or piped number** (#4800).
  `path(foreach (1,2) as $i (.; (. as {a:$q} | .), limit(0/1; empty); null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` twice in jq (the count cannot raise, so the `limit` stores nothing); it was refused naming `{"a":null}`, and `[path(...)?]` answered `[]` where jq answers `[["a"],["a"]]`. A count built from numeric literals under `/` and `%` by a divisor that is not zero (`limit(1/2; empty)`, `limit(7%2; empty)`, `limit(3%-3; empty)`) or piped into arithmetic on `.` (`limit(1|.-1; empty)`) beside the bind now matches over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A zero divisor (`1/0`, `0/0`, `1%0`, and `5%0.5`, whose divisor `%` truncates to `0`) and a state-dependent count (`limit(.; empty)`) keep their old reading.
