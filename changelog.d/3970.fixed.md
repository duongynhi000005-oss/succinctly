- **yq: a computed float slice bound that yq renders in exponent form is refused** (#3970). `.[(1e10*1):]` and `.[(1.0*1000000):]` sliced `[1,2]` to `[]` where yq raises `strconv.ParseInt: parsing "1e+10": invalid syntax`: yq hands the bound's rendered text to `ParseInt`, and Go's shortest `%g` switches to exponent form at a magnitude of `1e6` (`999999` is accepted, `-1000000` and `1234567` are not; a float past `i64` is refused too). `succinctly yq` now refuses any integral float of that size in a read, a write and a delete, with its usual `Array/string slice indices must be integers`. An integer product of any size is unchanged.
