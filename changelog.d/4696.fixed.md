- **yq: `line` and `column` after a stage that keeps the node answer the node's position** (#4696).
  `.a | map(.) | line` on `a: []` answered `0` where yq answers the line `.a` stands on, and the
  same for a scalar, an alias of an empty container and a mapping whose only entry is an empty
  `<<` merge. The gap was wider than `map`: `.a | del(.b) | line`, `.a | .b = 3 | column`,
  `sort`, `sort_by`, `flatten`, `sort_keys`, `map_values`, `pick`, `omit` and `. + {}`/`. * {}`
  all hand yq's own node on, so its line and column survive them, where a stage that builds a
  fresh node (`tostring`, `length`, `to_entries`, `[.]`, `unique`, `reverse`) reports `0` in
  both. Captured from yq v4.53.3 over a mapping, a sequence, an empty container, a scalar and an
  alias. `style`, `anchor` and the comments of a kept value, a `line` nested in a comma or an
  operand, and a child read through a write (`(.b = 3 | .b) | line`) still answer the owned
  default (recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`); `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
