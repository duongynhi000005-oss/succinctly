- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer under a `first`, `limit` or `nth(0; ..)`, an `if`, and a `select` of a truthy literal** (#4800).
  `del(foreach (1,2) as $i (.; first(. as {a:$q} | .); empty))` on `{"a":null}` is `{"a":null}` in jq (every output of the UPDATE is stored as the state and none is path-checked, so the answer is the EXTRACT's, and an `empty` one deletes nothing) and raised `Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}` here; with `$i` as EXTRACT the refusal named the UPDATE's own state (`result {"a":null}`) where jq names the EXTRACT's value (`result 1`). The same holds for `limit(N; ..)` (constant nonzero count, `-1` included), `nth(0; ..)`, `if . then B else . end` (a condition that cannot move the register, branches that are such binds, `.` or `empty`) and `select(1)`/`select("x")` ahead of or behind the bind: they now match over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A bind under a `//` (a falsy register would store the alternate), an `if` whose condition navigates or whose other branch stores a node of its own, `nth(1; ..)`, a state-dependent `limit` count and `select(.)` keep their refusal. Over a 53,760-row differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (42 UPDATE shapes x 8 sources x 5 EXTRACTs x `path`/`path?`/`del`/`|=` x 8 documents): 3,384 rows that were refused or worded differently now match, none that matched differ, and no row is wrongly accepted.
