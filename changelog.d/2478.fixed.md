- **`succinctly yq` field lookup through a `<<: [a, b]` merge-key sequence picks the later-listed source** (#2478). `.z.x` on `z: {<<: [*a, *b]}` answered `a`'s value; yq answers `b`'s, treating own keys, `<<` sources and their nested `<<` as one textual-order fold where the last write wins. `-o json '.z'` keeps its earlier-listed-wins output, which is what yq prints there too.
