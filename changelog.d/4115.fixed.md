- **jq: a `?//` bind whose destructure succeeds now retries the next alternative when the body leads with a navigation step** (#4115).
  `path(.x | (. as [$q] ?// $z | (.[0]?, true)))` on `{"x":[true]}` refused without printing anything where jq prints `["x",0]`
  and then raises `with result true`, and `. as [$q] ?// $z | .[0]` refused where jq answers `["x",0]` (`del`/`=` likewise). The
  destructure moves jq's register onto the bound node, so the body's first step on the ambient fails jq's own `path_intact`
  check, which is what sends jq to the next alternative; the resolver now retries on that refusal when the body leads with the
  navigation and the ambient is not equal to the moved register's value. Other body shapes keep their refusal. Applies to `succinctly jq` outside a `reduce`/`foreach` body, where the fold keeps its refusal.
