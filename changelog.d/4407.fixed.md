- **jq: a slice with both bounds absent is a syntax error, as in jq** (#4407). `succinctly jq '.[:]'` printed the whole array where jq 1.7.1 exits 3 with `syntax error, unexpected ']'`; the same held for `.[ : ]`, `.a[:]`, `.[:]?` and `path(.[:])`/`del(.[:])`. jq's grammar has no such slice (`.[:null]` and `.[null:]` are its spellings of the full slice, and are unchanged); jq mode now rejects it at parse time. `succinctly yq` still accepts `.[:]`, as yq does.
