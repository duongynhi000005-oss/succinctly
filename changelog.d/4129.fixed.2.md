- **jq: a `try ... catch` over a destructuring bind or fold no longer hides the register from the stage after it** (#4129, second part).
  `path(. as $x | {a:1} | (try (. as [$a] | $a) catch 7) | $x)` on `[{"a":1}]` and
  `path(. as $x | {a:{b:1}} | (try (reduce . as [$a] ?// $a (0; .)) catch 7) | $x)` raised `Invalid path expression with
  result` where jq answers `[]`, and `del`/`=` through such a `$x` refused the same way. A destructuring bind is now read
  from its body alone (its source is a subexpression and states nothing), and a `reduce`/`foreach` that destructures is
  read per result under a handler that navigates nothing. Over 13 new sweep operands (a seeded 15,000-row sample of the
  full grid, plus their 11,571 `--stage-only` rows) and a seeded 40,000-row `--stage-only` sample over the 506
  bind/fold/`catch` operands: REFUSE_WRONG 327 -> 233, 940 -> 888 and 648 -> 252, none lost, wrong accepts and wrong
  answers unchanged. A `try` handler that navigates, a body that holds a `.`, a fold entered under a `?//` bind (#4129
  item 3, which is a `?//` retry rule rather than a fold one) and a `foreach` UPDATE under a `?//` bind are still refused.
