  `a: 99999999999999999999 | .a | tostring` answered `1e+20` (and `"\(.a)"` the expanded `100000000000000000000.0`, and
  `.a = .a` rewrote the untouched field to that spelling, and `-r -o=json` printed the rounded double); yq answers the digits as written, `99999999999999999999`, while its JSON
  encoder still prints the double it parsed (`100000000000000000000.0`), which `-o=json` and `@json` keep doing. The digits ride
  with the value as its literal text and every yq JSON writer asks for the rounded spelling. The `+`-signed, leading-zero and explicitly tagged `!!float` spellings follow in #4921.
