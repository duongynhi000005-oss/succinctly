- **yq: the body of an `as` binding ends at a comma, and a comma over two reads of one variable keeps one** (#4183). A pipe binds tighter than `,` in yq, so `1 as $z | 2 as $y | $z, $y` is `(1 as $z | 2 as $y | $z), $y` and prints `1` (`$y` is unbound after the binding, which reads as nothing), `1 as $z | $z + 1, $z + 2` prints `2 2` and `true as $z | false or $z` is `false`; `succinctly yq` scoped the binding over the whole comma list and printed `1 2`, `2 3` and `true`. And `,` drops its right operand when both operands are the same list, which two reads of one variable are: `[1] as $z | $z, $z` is `[1]`, `[[1]] as $z | [$z, $z]` is `[[[1]]]`, `[1] as $z | [$z, $z, $z]` is `[[1],[1]]` (only the last pair collapses). jq mode is unchanged. A bare object entry over a repeated variable (`{$z, $z}`) stays recorded in the yq limitations.
