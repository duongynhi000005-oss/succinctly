- **yq: a second comment block after a blank line, following a foot block that closed a nested mapping, is the foot of the key that owns it** (#5053, item 2). `printf 'a:\n  b: 1\n  # A\n\n  # B\nc: 2\n' | yq .` is `a:\n  b: 1\n  # A\n# B\n\nc: 2\n` (`# A` stays on `.a.b`'s key; go-yaml ended the inner block at `# A`, so `# B` is `.a`'s, printed at column 0 with the blank line that separates it from `c`); succinctly kept `# B` at column 2 on `.b`. Also before a `---` and on `-P` and writes. It applies when exactly one blank line separates the blocks, the first sits at the open block's own column and the next key dedents past it; two blank lines, a first block at an odd column, a next key at the same level and a block at the end of the document keep the placement they had. Not fixed: a second block deeper than the first or than the open block (`    # B`), a third block (yq keeps the blank line between the second and third), and shapes nested more than two levels.
