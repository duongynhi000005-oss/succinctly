- **jq: a fold whose UPDATE is an `if`, a chain of `+`, or an append to a field no longer copies its state on every step** (#5279, #5273). Three fold shapes took the evaluator's bridge, or copied the array they extend, on every step: `reduce .[] as $x ([]; if $x.id % 3 == 0 then .[:-1] else . + [$x.id] end)` (an `if` whose condition is one value the step can settle, closed or reading the state, is a branch choice, and the chosen branch is a step of its own); `reduce .[] as $x (""; . + $x.name + ",")` (a left-associated chain `(. + a) + b` is applied link by link to the moved state); and `.l += [$x.id]`, `.l |= . + [$x.id]`, `.o += {k: v}` (the field is taken out of its slot, extended where it lies and put back, where it was copied because the state still held it). Any other pairing, a condition that raises, and a step that fails keep the evaluator's route and text. Measured on an Apple M5 Max (release builds, interleaved, min of 3, ~80 ms process-spawn floor included, output identical to jq 1.7.1): the `if` fold 0.24 s → 0.09 s at 5,000 records, 0.67 s → 0.08 s at 10,000, 2.57 s → 0.08 s at 20,000 (jq: 0.08 s); the string chain 0.20 s → 0.11 s, 0.52 s → 0.09 s, 1.98 s → 0.08 s (jq: 0.08 s); `.l += [$x.id]` 0.08 s → 0.08 s, 0.19 s → 0.08 s, 0.51 s → 0.08 s (jq: 0.40 s); `.o += {k: v}` 0.25 s → 0.09 s, 0.88 s → 0.09 s, 3.44 s → 0.09 s (jq: 1.48 s). A pipe of two assignments (`.l += [$x] | .n += 1`) still copies the field on every step.
