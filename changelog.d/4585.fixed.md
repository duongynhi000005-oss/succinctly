- **jq: a `try ... catch` around a `?//` chain of destructures of the register in a fold source inside another fold's body now raises where jq raises** (#4585).
  `path(. as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} ?// [$a] | .) catch .) as $y (.; .; .); .))` on `[true]` is `Invalid path expression with result [true]` in jq (exit 5)
  and printed `[]` twice here, and `del(...)` of it deleted the document. A matched alternative moves jq's register, so the code after the chain raises and `?//` retries the next
  alternative; the last has none to retry onto. A last alternative that type-errors lands in the `try` and the chain is answered (`{"a":true}` above, and `{b:$a} ?// {a:$a}` on `[1]`);
  one that matches leaves the raise uncaught (`[true]` above, `[$a] ?// {a:$a}` on `{"a":true}`); a bare `$v` alternative moves nothing and ends the chain answered. The refusal reads the accumulator's type
  per alternative (`caught_chain_raises`). Chains with a nested pattern, and a `try`/`?` without a handler, are unchanged. In `scripts/jq-fold-body-register-grid-sweep.py` the 24 `try-chain-catch` `ACCEPT_WRONG` rows
  are gone with no lost match against `main`.
