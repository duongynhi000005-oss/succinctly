- **yq: `to_json`, `to_json(N)`, `to_yaml` and `to_yaml(N)`** (#4206).
  `{"a": [1]} | to_json` answers the JSON text with two spaces of indent and a trailing line break, `to_json(0)` the
  compact text with none, and `to_yaml` / `to_yaml(N)` the `@yaml` encoder (yq reads an indent of `0` as four spaces and
  `1` as two). The indent must be a non-negative integer literal directly after the name, as in yq. Before, each answered
  `to_json/0 is not defined`. yq's own `tojson` (indented in yq) is still compact here, as recorded in
  `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
