- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure `foreach` behind a literal stage (`1 | FE`) as a `foreach` or `reduce` UPDATE is refused or emitted as jq does** (#4782, #4776).
  With `P = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)` on `{"a":{"b":1}}`, `path(.a as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (.; 1 | P; .))` and the same with `1 | P` as the EXTRACT emit nothing in jq and raised `result 1` here, and `reduce (1,2) as $i (.; 1 | P)` raises `result null` where this said `result 1`.
  A literal stage hands the `foreach` a value that is not the state and does not move jq's register, so the markers are now compared with the register the frame names and not with the literal: a `null` `$x` (`.a? as $x` on a `null` document) is still the register by kind, so `del`/`|=` through it still refuse where jq does.
