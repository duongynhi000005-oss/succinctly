- **yq: `.[]`, `.[] | key` and `map(.)` over a mapping with a sequence merge list its members in yq's iteration order and values** (#4983).
  With `a: &a {x: 1, z: 9}` and `b: &b {y: 2, z: 8}`, `item: {<<: [*a, *b], k: 0}` iterates `x, z, y, k` with values `1, 8, 2, 0` in yq (sources in listed order, each field upserting in place) while `succinctly yq` listed `y, z, x, k` with `2, 9, 1, 0`: it used the rendering's expansion, a sequence folded in reverse, for both. The merged field list is now two views: the rendering (`-o=json`, `tojson`, a nested value, the owned conversion) keeps the reverse fold, and everything that iterates reads the listed-order one; a single source, `[*a]` and a mapping without a merge key read the same in both. 65 rows over 13 merge shapes (an own key before, after and between, two `<<` keys, three sources) captured from yq v4.53.3 in `tests/yq_cli/i4983.rs`. `succinctly jq` over YAML shares the layer and now iterates the same way. Still different (recorded in `limitations.md`): yq's JSON rendering leaves an inline-mapping merge source out (#4987), `path` of a merged member, and `to_entries` (#4954).
