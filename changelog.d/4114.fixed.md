- **jq: `path(isempty(g))` answers through a `?//` that swallows its `first`'s break** (#4114). jq defines `isempty(g)` as `first((g | false), true)`, so a `?//` inside `g` catches the break after the first `false` (or the path error the consumer raises on it) and retries its next alternative; a retry that comes up dry leaves `g` running to its end and fires the trailing `true`. The resolver stopped at `g`'s first output, so `path(.x | isempty(. as $q ?// $z | if $q then true else empty end))` on `{"x":true}` was refused with `Invalid path expression with result false` where jq prints `["x"]`, and `del`/`|=` of it exited 5 where jq writes. `isempty` now hands each answer to the consumer from inside the drain, as `any`/`all` do (#3827, #3899): one `false` per answering alternative, the consumer's side effects ahead of the retry's, and the identity `true` once `g` ran to its end. yq mode is unchanged.
