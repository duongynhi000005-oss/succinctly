- **jq: a `foreach` source whose bound expression is `(.|.)` is checked like `. as {a:$a}`** (#4320, third part).
  `del(foreach ((.|.) as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .))` deleted the document where jq raises `Invalid path expression`, and
  `=`/`|=` through it wrote the same way. A pipe of `.` is the register as a bare `.` is: the source is routed to the
  resolver, which now reads a pipe whose stages are each the register as the register (the earlier attempt routed it
  without that, so a `try` around the fold swallowed the refusal). The error now carries jq's message and escapes a
  `try ... catch` as jq's does. Single pattern only: a `?//` chain keeps its retry rules. Over 99,603 sweep rows of every operand with a `?//` or a `(.|.)`, wrong accepts go from 327 to 215 and wrong answers from 141 to 131 with no match lost. A later bare `?//` alternative is still accepted (#4320).
