- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure `foreach` that is the UPDATE of a `reduce` whose source moves the register (`..`, `recurse`, `(.,.a)`) is refused, as jq refuses it** (#4777, #4754).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce .. as $i (.; (. | foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .))))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` raises `Invalid path expression with result null` in jq and answered `[]` here, as did the `// 5`, `select(.)`, `if . then FE else . end`, `try`, `first` and `label` wrappers
  (`// 5` raises `result 5`), and the `del`/`|=` through them wrote. The register sits on the source's element on every step: a step whose element is another node than `$x`'s raises (the `try` catches it and the `foreach` emits nothing, leaving the state `null`), and a step whose element *is* `$x`'s node
  raises nothing and keeps the state as it found it, so one raising step is enough when the `foreach` hands its state on unchanged (`INIT`, `UPDATE` and `EXTRACT` all `.`). A source whose every element is `$x`'s node (`.a`, `(.a,.a)`, `.[]` on one key) or that yields nothing (`empty`) is still answered.
  A `foreach` whose body changes the state, and a `null`/boolean `$x` (it compares by kind, not position) in such a body, are as they were.
