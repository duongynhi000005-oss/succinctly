- **jq: a `foreach` with a navigating source keeps the register for EXTRACT behind a comma of `isempty`/`any`/`all` UPDATEs and a `first`/`limit`-wrapped operand** (#4623).
  `path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; (isempty(1), isempty(2)); $k))` on `[1]` is `[0]` twice in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result 1` here, and so were `(isempty(1), 5)`,
  `isempty(first(1))`, `isempty(limit(1; 1, 2))` and `any(first(1); .)`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. jq forks a comma and backtracks an operand, so the register is where UPDATE
  entered whenever every item and operand leaves it there (#4611); a sibling or operand that navigates (`(isempty(1), isempty(.a?))`, `isempty(first(.a?))`) is still refused, as in jq.
