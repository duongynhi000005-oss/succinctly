- **jq: a top-level `reduce` source that destructures a fresh value now raises where jq raises when the fresh value reaches the destructure through a pipe, a parameterised call, a `//` operand or a `?//` bare alternative** (#4566).
  #4522 left these spellings to the by-value drive, which answered where jq exits 5 (`near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":1}`), so `del(...)` of them printed `null` or `{}` (the document deleted) and `... |= 9` replaced it: `{a:1} | . as {a:$a} | .`, `f as $x | $x | . as {a:$a} | .`, `keys | . as [$a] | .`, `def f(x): {a:1}; f(.) as {a:$a} | .`, `def g(x): x; g(f) as {a:$a} | .`, `(f as {a:$a} | .) // .`, `null // (f as {a:$a} | .)`, `f as $x ?// $y | $y as {a:$a} | .` and a fresh destructure after a destructure of the register (`g(.) | g({a:1})`) now raise. A destructure jq never runs (`empty | ...`, `select(false) | ...`, the right side of `. // ...`), the register behind a call and a variable bound before the fresh value are still answered, as in jq. Still accepted: a call whose `def` hands the register on next to a fresh value, a `reduce` source inside another fold's body, and a destructure of a variable bound to the document after a path-expression INIT moved the register (`. as $x | f as $y | $y | $x as {a:$a} | .` with INIT `.a`).
