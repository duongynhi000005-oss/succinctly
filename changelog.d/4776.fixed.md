- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure in a `foreach` source that is wrapped in a fallback or `if . then ... else . end` in a `foreach` UPDATE, or is a `foreach` EXTRACT, behaves as jq does** (#4776, #4754).
  With `P = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)` on `{"a":{"b":1}}`, `path(.a as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (.; (. | P) // 3; .))` raises `Invalid path expression with result 3` in jq and printed `[] []` here; `if . then P else . end` raises `result null` (the step after the `null` an empty one leaves behind takes the `else`; a one-item source emits nothing); `P` as the EXTRACT emits nothing. The `=`/`|=`/`del` forms refuse as jq does. A `1 | P` stage is unchanged.
