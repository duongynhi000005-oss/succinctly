- **jq: a `try`/`?` around a foreign-`$x` destructure in a `foreach` source followed by a pipe stage, as a `reduce` UPDATE, is refused as jq refuses it** (#4724, #4709).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) | .))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` raises `Invalid path expression with result null` in jq and printed `[]` here (`del(...)` deleted the document, `=`/`|=` replaced it). The caught destructure leaves the `foreach` empty and the pipe's later stages run once per output, so the `reduce` state is `null`. A comma beside the `foreach` (`, 1`) and a handler that emits are unchanged.
