- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that binds `.` with a single pattern keeps jq's answer beside an `empty` that is one only by evaluation** (#4800).
  `path(foreach (1,2) as $i (.; (. as {a:$q} | .), select(false|not|not); null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` twice in jq (a `select` whose condition is never truthy stores nothing, so the state is the bind's output alone); it was refused naming `{"a":null}`, and `[path(...)?]` answered `[]` where jq answers `[["a"],["a"]]`. A `select` of a constant that is never truthy (`false|not|not`, `false,null`, `empty`, `false // null`) and a `limit` whose count is built from numeric literals under `+`, `-`, `*` (`limit(1+0; empty)`, `limit(0+0; ..)`, `limit(-1+0; empty)`) beside the bind now match over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. A condition or count that can raise (`select(error)`, `limit(1/0; empty)`) or depends on the state (`select(.a)`, `limit(.a.b; empty)`) keeps its old reading.
