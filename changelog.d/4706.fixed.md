- **jq: a composed `isempty(recurse)` / `any(recurse; .)` (`| not`, `and`, a `def`) as a `foreach` UPDATE keeps the register** (#4706, #4698).
  `[path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; (isempty(recurse) | not); $k))?]` on `[1]` is `[[0]]` in jq and was `[]` here (the refusal was swallowed by the `?` and the stream element dropped; without the `?` it raised `Invalid path expression with result 1`), as were `(isempty(recurse) and true)`, `(any(recurse; .) | not)`, `(isempty(..) | not)` and `isempty(def f: recurse; f)`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. A recursion's seed is its first output, at the register, so a verdict that stops there (`isempty`, or `any` over `.` of an f-less recursion, whose falsy seed has no children) leaves the register where it entered whatever wraps it. A verdict that can end on a descendant (`any(recurse(f); .)`, `any(recurse; true)`, `all(recurse; .)`) is unchanged and still refused.
