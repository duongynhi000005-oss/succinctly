- **yq: `sort` and `sort_by(f)` on a mapping reorder its entries instead of raising jq's pairing error** (#2064). yq sorts a mapping by its values (or by `[f]` of each value), stably and with the comparator it uses for an array, and answers a mapping: `{"a":2,"b":1} | sort` is `{"b":1,"a":2}` and `{"a":{"x":2},"b":{"x":1},"c":{"y":9}} | sort_by(.x)` is `{"c":{"y":9},"b":{"x":1},"a":{"x":2}}`. `succinctly yq` raised `object (...) and array (...) cannot be sorted, as they are not both arrays`; `succinctly jq` still does, as jq does. The sorted mapping is an owned value, so YAML output drops its comments, flow style and anchors and writes a numeric key as a string; a mapping with a repeated key is refused rather than losing an entry (recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`).
