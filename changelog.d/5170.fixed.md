- **jq: a bind of an outer `$x` with a comma sibling inside a `//` in a `foreach` or `reduce` UPDATE no longer refuses on the step after the first** (#5170). `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (($x as {a:$a} | .), 7) // 5; $i))` on `null` is `["a"]`, `["a"]`, `[]` in jq and was `["a"]` then `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of null` (exit 5) here, as were the sibling ahead of the bind (`(7, B) // 5`), a `null` sibling, a comma of constants and the writes through them (`|= 9`, `del`). The sibling runs from the register the comma was entered with, so a bind-free sibling that cannot move the register is one more item of the sequence the register is carried through (#5157); a bind whose own body emits more than once (`(B | (.,.)), 7`, `(B | range(2)), 7`) stays refused, since the resolver delivers only the first output of a generator body when the state is not the register (#5193; a comma of dots was #5185). Over about 77,000 sampled grid rows for a three-argument `foreach`, 66,150 for a two-argument `foreach` and 66,150 for a `reduce` (`scripts/jq-fold-update-alt-bind-sweep.py`, now with the sibling shapes and the tails `(.,.)` and `range(2)`) against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 1,325, 545 and 1,258 rows that were refused, answered wrongly or worded the error differently now match, none that matched differ and none turned a refusal into a wrong answer. Still refused where jq answers: a `?//` chain behind the `//` in a three-argument `foreach` (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`).
