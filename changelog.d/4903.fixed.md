- **`succinctly yq` reads a text argument with no output as the empty string** (#4903). In yq `"a1b" | split(.foo)` is `["a","1","b"]`, `test(.foo)` is `true`, `capture(.foo)` is `{}`, `sub(.foo; "X")` is `"XaX1XbX"` and `sub("a"; select(false))` is `"1b"`, because an absent key or `select(false)` stands for `""`; `succinctly yq` printed nothing for each, as jq does. This covers the separator, pattern, flags and replacement of `split`, `test`, `match`, `capture` and `sub`, and settles `"a1b" | split(.nan)` (#4374), where `.nan` is a field access on a string. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
