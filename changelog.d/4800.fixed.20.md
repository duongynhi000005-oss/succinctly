- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` under a `label`, with comma siblings that emit nothing, in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source, keeps jq's answer** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; label $l | ((foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)), select(false)); .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq and was refused naming the state (`Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`), and `[path(..)?]` answered `[]` where jq answers `[["a"]]`; the bare `label $l | FE` and a `label` over a comma whose other items are `empty`, `select(false)`, `first(empty)` or a `break $l` behind the `foreach` were the same. The reading that names the register looked through a `label` for neither, and the `label` arm of the emptied-update reading dropped only a literal `empty`: both now take the one item the `label` scope runs, and decline a `break $l` ahead of it or inside it (`path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`, with `.`, `$i`, `empty` and `.a` EXTRACTs). An `if . then FE else . end` UPDATE under such a `label`, or beside comma items that emit nothing (`(if . then FE else . end), empty`), is now read as the bare `if` is since #4948: on `{"a":null}` and `{"a":true}` its `$i` EXTRACT was refused naming the state where jq's `path` answers `[]`, and on `{"a":null}` a `.a` EXTRACT under a `?` let the index error escape where jq answers `[]`. Over a 13,840-row differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and `main` (173 label spellings x 4 EXTRACTs x 5 documents x 4 wrappers): 768 rows that were refused or worded differently now match and no wrong answer is newly accepted; 13 rows (all on `{"a":{"a":null}}`, a `.a` EXTRACT under a `?`) regress from a refusal that happened to answer `[]` like jq to the bare `foreach`'s own refusal, which names jq's index error but is not caught by the `?` (documented in `limitations.md`). An 840-row grid of the `if` spellings (14 UPDATEs x 4 EXTRACTs x 5 documents x 3 wrappers) moves 122 rows to jq's answer and none away from it.
