- **yq: a float in scientific notation prints without allocating** (#4923). `format_float_yq`'s scientific branch built `{f:e}`, a mantissa `String` for the tie-break correction and the final `mantissa e sign exp` `String`: three to four allocations per number, which #4917 had removed only for ordinary magnitudes. The streaming printer now formats the digits into a stack buffer, asks the tie-break question about them (`shortest_decimal_is_wrong_tie`, the decision `correct_shortest_decimal_tiebreak` already made, split out so it needs no `String`) and writes the `e+NN` spelling piece by piece. Output is byte-identical (JSON and YAML, 8 MB of mixed numbers) and `format_float_yq` stays the definition the tests compare against, including the exact decimal ties past the threshold. Interleaved A/B, release builds, 15 runs, `yq -p json -o json .` on an Apple M5 Max (instructions retired, median): 2 MB with 20% scientific numbers 0.950 G to 0.892 G (-6.1%), 8 MB 3.700 G to 3.464 G (-6.4%), 8.8 MB all scientific 5.172 G to 3.999 G (-22.7%; wall -15%), and 7.9 MB with none 3.333 G to 3.333 G (0.0%). Not measured on x86_64.
