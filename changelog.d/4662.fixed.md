- **jq: a `reduce` UPDATE of `limit(n; CHAIN)` for a count above one answers in `path()` as `first(CHAIN)` does** (#4662).
  `path(reduce (1,2) as $i (.; limit(2; . as {a:$a} ?// $a | .)))` on `{"a":true}` raised `Invalid path expression` where jq prints `[]`, and so did `limit(1,2; CHAIN)`. A count of one reached the accumulator only because the bounded sink's stop forced the `?//` retry; a larger count never stops there, so the step saw the destructured alternative's untracked `.`. A `?//` chain whose alternatives all hand `.` back under a `first`/`limit` now keeps the accumulator as the register's node. A body that is not `.`, `nth` and a zero count stay refused, as in jq.
