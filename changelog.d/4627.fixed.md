- **jq: a caught empty-body destructure as a `foreach` source in a navigating `foreach`'s body no longer loses the loop element's path** (#4627). `path(. as $x | foreach .[]? as $i (.; foreach (try (. as [$a] | empty) catch .) as $y (.; .; .); $i))` on `[[1],[2]]` raised `Invalid path expression with result [1]` (exit 5) where jq 1.7.1 prints `[0]` and `[1]`; the `del` and `|=` forms refused the same way (jq: `[]`, `[[9,9]]`), and so did `try (. as [$a] | .) catch .`. The destructure raises on the accumulator (`.` is not the register), jq restores the register to where the `try` entered and the handler emits from there, so the inner fold's emission leaves the register on the element and `$i` is it; the fold read that emission as having lost the register. A `foreach` source of the form `try (. as P | ...) catch H`, `H` unable to move the register, is now read as leaving it at the fold's entry (`FoldRegister::advance`).
