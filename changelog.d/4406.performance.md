- **jq: a navigated literal slice read by a separate stage, `.a[2:] | length`, no longer builds the slice** (#4406). #4323 fused `.a[2:][0]` and `.a | .[2:] | length`, but `.a[2:]` followed by `| length`, `| first`, `| last` or `| .[k]` parses to a pipe whose first stage is the nested chain `.a[2:]`, which the fusion did not see, so every read converted the whole tail of the array. The fusion now looks through that one nested pipe when every stage before the slice is a pure path (`.a`, `.a.b`, `.a[0].b`), under a postfix `?` on the slice too, and the computed index `.a[2:][$k]` is covered with it. `[range(1000) as $i | .a[2:] | length] | length` over `{"a":[{"a":{"b":i}}, ...]}` took 936 ms for 2,000 elements and 1,871 ms for 4,000 on an Apple M4 Pro, now 3.9 ms and 4.4 ms; on a Ryzen 9 7950X (pinned to one core) 1,329 ms and 2,570 ms, now 2.5 ms and 2.7 ms. `first`, `last`, `.[k]` and `.a[2:][$k]` move the same way: 971-2,179 ms before, 4.3-4.9 ms after (M4 Pro, 2,000 and 4,000 elements). The old time doubled per doubling; the new one grows 1.1x. Release builds, interleaved min of 7, output identical to the old binary on every row, and the fused controls (`.a | .[2:] | length`, `.a[$i:] | length`, `.a[2:][0]`) unchanged; instruction counts on the perf-guard matrix move +0.0% (7950X). Like the head form, the fused read converts only the element it names (`limitations.md`, "A fused slice read does not validate the rest of the range"); a parenthesised `(.a[2:]) | length` and a path that can fan out or fail quietly (`.a[]?[2:]`) keep the ordinary route.
