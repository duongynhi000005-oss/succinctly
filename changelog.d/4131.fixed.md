- **jq: a `reduce` on an untracked entry follows an `UPDATE` that navigates a `null`/boolean accumulator** (#4131).
  `path(.a | 5 | reduce null as {a:$q} (null; .b))` on `{"a":null}` refused at `.b` ("of null") where jq answers `["a"]`.
  `null` and the booleans are `jv_identical` to an equal one by kind, so an accumulator holding one is on the register the
  stage carries (#4049) and the step passes `path_intact`; a boolean accumulator now raises jq's own `Cannot index boolean`,
  and `del`/`=` through the fold land on `.a`. A register that is not `null`/boolean and a fold that mentions a frozen `$var` still refuse where jq answers (the safe direction).
