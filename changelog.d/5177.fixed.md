- **jq: a nested `foreach` whose source destructures an outer `$x` on a step whose state is the register raises catchably** (#5177). With INIT `.`, `try (foreach ($x as [$a] | .) ...) catch $i` ended the path `with result` the root (an error nothing catches) where jq's pattern raises and the `try` runs its handler; the unguarded form's message was wrong too. `path`, `del`, `|=` and `[path(..)?]` now match jq.
