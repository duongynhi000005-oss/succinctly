  `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; if . then (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .), empty) else . end; $i))` on `{"a":null}` is `[]` in jq and was refused naming the state (`Invalid path expression with result null`), and the same shape under a `?` answered `[]` for jq's `[[]]`; `del` and `|=` answered or refused alike. A comma `then` branch whose other items emit nothing (`empty`, `select(false)`, `first(empty)`, on either side of the `foreach`) is now read as the `foreach` alone, as the bare `if` has been since #4948.
