- **`succinctly yq` no longer raises when a write's scalar-literal branch is followed by navigation** (#1868). `(.a, 1 | .[]) = 5`, `(.a, 1 | .b) = 5`, `(.a, "s" | .[0]) = 5`, `(.a, 1 | .[0:1]) = 5` and `del(.a, 1 | .[])` raised jq's "Invalid path expression"; real yq has no such notion, leaves the document alone for that branch and writes every other (`a: 5`, `b: 2`). `=`, `|=`, the compound operators and `del()` now match. A bound `$x`, a computed value, a literal array or object, a computed key and `..` keep the refusal (`(.a, 1 | ..) = 5`, `(.a, 1 | .[$k]) = 5` still differ from yq), and `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
