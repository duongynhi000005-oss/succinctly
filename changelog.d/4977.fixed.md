- **yq: `@props` escapes keys and values the way yq's properties encoder does** (#4977).
  `{"a":"x\ny"} | @props` is `a = x\ny` (backslash, `n`) in yq; `succinctly yq` replaced the newline with a space ("as yq does", which was wrong) and wrote a tab, a backslash, a key's space and `:` raw. The table, captured from yq v4.53.3 over every control character, backslash, space, `:`, `=`, `#`, `!` and non-ASCII for key and value separately (420 cases, all matching): backslash, tab, newline, carriage return and form feed become `\\`, `\t`, `\n`, `\r`, `\f`; a key also escapes every space and `:` (`a\ b = 1`, `a\:b = 1`); a value escapes only a leading space (`a = \  x`); other controls, `=`, `#`, `!`, `.` and non-ASCII are written as they are; a root scalar is not escaped at all, and a scalar whose joined key is empty (`{"":"v"}`) writes nothing, as in yq (it printed the value). 47 rows in `tests/yq_cli/i4977.rs` and three in `tests/jq_cli/i4977.rs` (the terminator is trimmed on both sides: the trailing newline is #1076's). `succinctly jq` shares `@props` as a yq-flavoured extension, so it follows.
