- **jq: a fold source after a navigating INIT runs on the register INIT moved** (#5017). Outside every fold body, after INIT `.a`, a `$x` bound at `.a` is now jq's register in SOURCE: `path(.a as $x | foreach ($x | .c) as $y (.a; .; $y))` is `["a","c"]` as in jq (it raised), and a destructure of it refuses where jq does (it answered `["a"]`, and `del` deleted `.a`). A caught destructure of a `$x` bound elsewhere, behind a stage (`try ($x | . as {b:$q} | .)`), a `label`, a `select(true)`, or beside a comma item, empties its item as jq's `try` does, where `del` deleted `.a`. The same holds on every INIT fork and for a destructure spelled through `.` stages (`try ($x | . | . as P | .)`), which also answers as jq does inside another fold's body. A `reduce` nested in such a source (`foreach (reduce ($x as {b:$q} | .) as $w (0; .)) as $y (.a; .; .)`) and a fold behind a computed stage (`1 | reduce ..`, `.a | tostring | reduce ..`) now raise at a foreign `$x`'s destructure too, where `del` deleted `.a`.
