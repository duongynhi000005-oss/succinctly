- **jq: a fold source after a navigating INIT runs on the register INIT moved** (#5017). Outside every fold body, after INIT `.a`, a `$x` bound at `.a` is now jq's register in SOURCE: `path(.a as $x | foreach ($x | .c) as $y (.a; .; $y))` is `["a","c"]` as in jq (it raised), and a destructure of it refuses where jq does (it answered `["a"]`, and `del` deleted `.a`). A caught destructure of a `$x` bound elsewhere, behind a stage (`try ($x | . as {b:$q} | .)`), a `label`, a `select(true)`, or beside a comma item, empties its item as jq's `try` does, where `del` deleted `.a`. The same holds on every INIT fork and for a destructure spelled through `.` stages (`try ($x | . | . as P | .)`), which also answers as jq does inside another fold's body.
