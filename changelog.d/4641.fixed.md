- **jq: a `foreach` with a navigating source keeps the register for EXTRACT behind an `isempty`/`any`/`all` UPDATE over a `path()`, a `select`, a leading `empty`, an `and`/`or` of verdicts or a `def` wrapper** (#4641).
  `path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; isempty(path(.[]?)); $k))` on `[1]` is `[0]` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result 1` here, as were `isempty(empty | .a)`, `isempty(1) and isempty(2)`, `any(true; select(.))`, `all(1; first(.))` and `def f: isempty(empty); f`, with the `del(...)`/`=` twins. Under `?`/`try` the refusal was swallowed and the stream element dropped (`[path(...)?]` was `[]` where jq prints `[[0]]`). An `any`/`all` `cond` that navigates nothing (`first(.)`, `select(f)`) is now evaluated by value, so an element equal to the register no longer reads as an ambiguous guess. A verdict next to a navigating comma item or `and`/`or` side (`(isempty(1), isempty(.a?))`) is still refused, as before.
