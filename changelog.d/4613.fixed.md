- **jq: a `reduce` whose source is a destructuring `?//` chain now answers on an untracked entry, as jq does** (#4613).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (reduce ((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .)) | $x)` on `[[1],[1]]` is `[]` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result [[1],[1]]` here, as was
  the `(.|1) as ...` form and the `del(...)` twin (`del(. as $x | 1 | (reduce ((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | $a) as $k (.; $k)) | $x.a?)`, jq: the document). jq backtracks a `reduce`'s source, so
  the register it leaves is `INIT`'s whichever alternative bound; the fold now states it when its source moves it only through a bind that ends in a bare `$var` (the gate #4489 added for `foreach`), in a fold that is not
  inside another fold's body. A navigating `UPDATE`, a source that navigates before its bind and a chain with no bare alternative are still refused, as in jq. Over the 36 `reduce` operands with a `?//`/destructuring source (`--stage-only`, 62,037 rows, `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1, against a `main` build) `MATCH` went 58,365 to 58,725 and `REFUSE_WRONG` 3,106 to 2,746, with no regression and `ACCEPT_WRONG` (554) and `DIFF` (12) unchanged.
