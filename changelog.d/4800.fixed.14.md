- **jq: a `foreach` whose EXTRACT emits nothing no longer refuses a nested `foreach` in its UPDATE that destructures a `$x` marker** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); empty))` on `{"a":1}` is nothing in jq (the EXTRACT never emits, so no state is path-checked); it was refused naming `{"a":1}`, and `[path(...)?]` was refused too where jq answers `[]`. An UPDATE that can neither raise nor have an effect (`.`, literals, `not`, `tostring`, `select(true)`, and a pipe, comma, `//`, `if`, `try`/`?` or nested `foreach` of such, with a destructuring pattern only inside a `try`/`?`) beside an EXTRACT of `empty`, `select(false)`, `first(empty)` or `limit(0; ..)` now matches over `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`: 144 of 2,160 grid rows against jq 1.7.1 went from a refusal to a match, with no wrong accept and no lost match. An uncaught destructure (`foreach ($x as {a:$a} | .) ...`), which raises or not by the state, keeps its refusal.
