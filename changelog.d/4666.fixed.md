- **jq: a fresh item behind a counted nested `limit` or a pipe that repeats its input, as a `reduce` source, no longer lets `path()`/`del`/`=` answer where jq raises** (#4666).
  `[path(reduce (limit(3; limit(2; ., .), {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .))]` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq (`{a:1}` is the third output, destructured) and was `[[]]` here, as was `(((., {a:1}) | ., .) | . as {a:$a} | .)`, with `del(...)` deleting the document and `=`/`|=` replacing it. The scan that decides whether a fresh comma item reaches the destructure now subtracts the outputs a wrapper over single-output items yields from the budget of the items after it, and reads a pipe whose later stages are `., .` as handing every output of the first stage on. Where the outputs cannot be counted the scan still stops, and the contrasts that never reach the fresh item (`limit(2; limit(2; ., .), {a:1})`) are unchanged.
