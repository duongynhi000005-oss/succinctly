- **jq: `foreach (limit(n; (CHAIN), X))` prints the register once in `path()` where the chain's refused first emission counts against `n`** (#4663).
  `path(foreach (limit(2; (. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .), 1)) as $k (.; .; .))` on `{"a":true}` printed `[]` twice where jq prints it once, and `limit(1; ...)`
  with a literal or `empty` sibling did the same. `limit(n; ..)` now reads a leading `?//` chain inside a comma the way `first((CHAIN), 1)` does (#4537), so the
  resolver counts the refused first emission against `n`; the nine-row `n` in 1..3 by sibling `1`/`.a`/`empty` matrix matches jq 1.7.1.
