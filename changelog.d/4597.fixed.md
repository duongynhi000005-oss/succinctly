- **jq: a `reduce` source whose fresh comma item sits behind a wrapper, a `reduce` stage or `isempty`/`any`/`all` now raises where jq raises** (#4597).
  `path(reduce (limit(2; ., {a:1}, .) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .))` on `{"a":5}` is `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":1}` in jq (exit 5) and
  printed `[[]]` here, and `del(...)` of it deleted the document and `=`/`|=` replaced it. The first part (a `first`/`limit(1; ..)`/`nth(0; ..)` reads only the operand's first output, so the fresh item has to be that one) is
  unchanged; the rest reads the wrappers as they generate (`RegisterSpine::fresh_among_leading`): `try`/`?` and `label` every item, `limit(n; ..)` and `first(({a:1}, .), 2)` the first n outputs,
  `nth(k; ..)` the one at index k, a pipe of one stage its operand, a `reduce . as $x (.; .)` stage hands `.` on, and `isempty(g)`/`any(g; c)`/`all(g; c)` run their generator where they stand
  (`({a:1}, .) | isempty(. as {a:$a} | .)` raises). Inside a wrapper an `error` or `break` ahead of the fresh item hides it, so `try (., error("x"), {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .` and
  `limit(2; ., ., {a:1})` still answer; the bare comma stage keeps reading what it can generate, as #4582 did.
