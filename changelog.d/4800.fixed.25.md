- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE pipe with a `select(false)` stage is read as the `empty` it is, and the stages after the first stage that emits nothing are dropped** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; (if .a then (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .), empty) else . end) | select(false); .))` on `{"a":true}` is nothing in jq (the UPDATE never emits, so `path` emits nothing and `del`/`|=` echo the document) and was refused naming the state, uncatchably under `?`, while the same UPDATE ending on `empty` was already answered. A stage that emits nothing (`select(false)`, `empty`, any `select` of a constant that is never truthy) ends the pipe, so `FE | select(false) | .a` and `FE | empty | .a` are read as the UPDATE that never emits too. Against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1, on the 2,520-row slice of the 70,560-row `if`-UPDATE grid of #5029 that holds this spelling (`(if .a then (FE, empty) else . end) | select(false)`: 6 sources x 7 EXTRACTs x 5 write contexts x 12 documents) 420 rows differed and 360 are fixed, none lost, none newly accepted wrongly; the 60 left are the `null` EXTRACT behind an `if`, recorded in `limitations.md`.
