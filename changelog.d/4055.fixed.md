- **yq: `min` and `max` of an array follow yq's comparator instead of jq's total order** (#4055, #4236).
  `[2, "10", 3] | min` printed `2` and yq stops with `!!str not yet supported for comparison`; the same for booleans (`[true, false] | min`), containers (`arrays`/`maps not yet supported for comparison`, `!!seq ([0]) cannot be subtracted from !!int`) and any two scalars of different kinds. A `null` element never takes part (`[null, 1] | min` is `null`, `[1, null]` is `1`, where jq sorts `null` first) and a tie keeps the first element for `max` as well (`[1, 1.0] | max` is `1`). The route over a document's own array and the one over a computed array agree. The values of a mapping (`{a: true, b: false} | min`) and `min_by`/`max_by` keep jq's ordering.
