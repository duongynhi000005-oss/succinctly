- **jq: a `foreach` whose source is a destructuring `?//` chain now answers on an untracked entry, as jq does** (#4489).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach ((.|1) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .)) | $x)` on `[[1],[1]]` is `[]` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result [[1],[1]]` here, and so were
  `(.|.) as ...`, `(., .) | . as ...` and the `del(...)` twin (`del(. as $x | 1 | (foreach ((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | $a) as $k (.; $k; .)) | $x.a?)`, jq: the document). The pattern's first `INDEX`
  raises on a value that is not the register, `?//` restores the path and the bare alternative's element leaves the register where the fold entered; the fold's emission now states that
  register when its source moves it only through such a bind (also behind a plain `as`, a `label`, an `if` and `last(f)`) and the element took no walk step. A body, `UPDATE` or `EXTRACT` that
  navigates, a source that navigates before its bind and a chain with no bare alternative are still refused, as in jq. Over the 91 `foreach` operands with a `?//` source (`--stage-only`, 20,027 rows,
  `--sample 20000 --seed 4489`, against a `main` build): `MATCH` 18,803 -> 19,053, `REFUSE_WRONG` 1,156 -> 906, `ACCEPT_WRONG` (15) and `DIFF` (53) unchanged.
  Sources through `first`/`limit`/`nth`, a `def` call with arguments or a nested `foreach`, and the `reduce` twin are still refused.
