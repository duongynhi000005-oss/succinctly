- **jq: a two-alternative `?//` chain as a `foreach` source inside a carrying `reduce`, in the body of a fold whose source moved the register, is refused as jq raises** (#4651).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $k (.; reduce 1 as $j (.; foreach ((.|.) as {a:$a} ?// [$a] | .) as $k (.; .; .))))` on `[true]` exits 5 in jq (`Invalid path expression near attempt to access element 0 of [true]`) and was `null` here, as were the `path()`, `reduce .[1+1]`, `reduce first` and outer-`foreach` UPDATE spellings; `=`/`|=` replaced the document. A nested `reduce` whose source cannot move the register and whose INIT is `.` now carries its enclosing body's moved-register mark (a `reduce` source that navigated, or a `foreach` source that did) into its own UPDATE, where `.` is still the outer state. The same fold where `.` is the register (`reduce 1 as $j (.; ...)` at the top, or under `foreach (1,2)`) is unchanged.
