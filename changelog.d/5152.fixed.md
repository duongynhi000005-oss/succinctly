- **jq: `$ARGS` lists `positional` before `named`, as jq does** (#5152). `jq -nc --arg a 1 '$ARGS' --args x y` is `{"positional":["x","y"],"named":{"a":"1"}}` in jq 1.7.1 and printed `{"named":..,"positional":..}` here, so `$ARGS` shown whole, `$ARGS | keys_unsorted`, `to_entries` and `tojson` differed (`keys`, `.positional` and `.named.x` did not).
