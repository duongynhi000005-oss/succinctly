- **jq: a `//` over a bind of an outer `$x` in a `reduce` or `foreach` UPDATE is read when its right operand holds binds of its own** (#5175). #5167 read the right operand only when it held no bind; one with a bind (`.a as $q | 1`, `$x as {a:$a} | .`) kept the resolver's refusal, and a `try`/`?` around the `//` swallowed it, so the UPDATE emitted nothing and `path`/`del`/`|=` answered (or wrote) where jq raises at the emission of the left operand's truthy output. `path(. as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (false; try (($x as {a:$a} | 5) // .a as $q | 1)))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` and `(. as $x | reduce (.a,.) as $i (5; try (($x as {a:$a} | .) // (.a as $q | $q)))) |= 9` on `null` are `Invalid path expression with result 5` (exit 5) in jq; they answered `[]`/nothing and `9` here. jq runs the right operand from the register the `//` was entered with and runs a bind's source in a subexpression that tracks no path, so a right operand that is a bare chain of binds (one literal-key pattern each, a source that is `.`, an outer `$x` or a navigation of `.`) behind a left operand with no comma or fold is now read like the left one. Over the grid rows with such an operand (`scripts/jq-fold-update-alt-bind-sweep.py`, sampled, against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build) 482 of 15,120 two-argument `foreach`, 165 of 15,120 `reduce` and 1,850 of 60,480 three-argument `foreach` wrong accepts now match jq, and no row that matched before differs. Still a wrong accept (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`): an operand with a `def` or a call, a comma, an `if` or a `?//` chain around its binds, a generator or computed source, one behind a left operand with a comma or a fold (#5170), a `?//` chain behind the `//` in a `reduce`, and still refused where jq answers, a `reduce` whose final accumulator is the register's node.
