- **yq: `split({})` and the error for a non-string input now match yq** (#4374). `"a{}b" | split({})` is `["a","b"]` in yq (the `{}` *literal*'s node carries the text `{}`) and was `["a","{","}","b"]` here; a `{}` read from a document still splits into characters in both. `5 | split(",")` now raises `cannot split !!int, can only split strings` (with `!!bool`, `!!float`, `!!seq`, `!!map` for the other kinds) instead of the jq wording; `succinctly jq` keeps its own. `.nan`/`.inf` as separator literals (parsed as field accesses) and `split({} | .)` stay open and are recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
