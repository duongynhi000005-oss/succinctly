- **jq: a top-level `reduce` source that destructures a fresh value now raises where jq raises for a `?//` chain, a `(def f: ...; f)` bound expression, a variable, a closure argument and a path-expression INIT** (#4522).
  #4494 routed `reduce (def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .)` through the resolver; these spellings of the same destructure still took the by-value drive, which answered where jq exits 5 (`near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":1}`), so `del(...)` of them printed `null` or `{}` (the document deleted) and `... |= 9` replaced it. `def f: {a:1}; f as [$a] ?// {a:$a} | .`, `(def f: {a:1}; f) as {a:$a} | .`, `f as $x | $x as {a:$a} | .`, `def g(x): x; g(def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .)` and `reduce SRC as $k (.a; .)` (the source raises whatever INIT left the register on) now raise. A chain with a bare `$var` alternative to retry onto, the register behind a variable or a closure argument, and the value-mode spelling are still answered, as in jq. Still accepted: a call whose `def` hands the register on next to a fresh value (`def f: ., {a:1}`), a `reduce` source inside another fold's body, and a `null` source. (A fresh value piped into a destructure, a parameterised call as the bound expression, a `//` operand and a `?//` bare alternative the body destructures were the rest of this list; #4566 fixed them.)
