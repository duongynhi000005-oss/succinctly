- **jq: a `reduce` source that destructures a fresh value now raises where jq raises for a `?//` chain, a `(def f: ...; f)` bound expression, a variable, a closure argument, a path-expression INIT, a call whose `def` hands the register on next to a fresh value, and a `reduce` inside another fold's body** (#4522).
  #4494 routed `reduce (def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .)` through the resolver; these spellings of the same destructure still took the by-value drive, which answered where jq exits 5 (`near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":1}`), so `del(...)` of them printed `null` or `{}` (the document deleted) and `... |= 9` replaced it. `def f: {a:1}; f as [$a] ?// {a:$a} | .`, `(def f: {a:1}; f) as {a:$a} | .`, `f as $x | $x as {a:$a} | .`, `def g(x): x; g(def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .)` and `reduce SRC as $k (.a; .)` (the source raises whatever INIT left the register on) now raise. A chain with a bare `$var` alternative to retry onto, the register behind a variable or a closure argument, and the value-mode spelling are still answered, as in jq. `def f: ., {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .` raises too: the register output is jq's own, since a `reduce` backtracks its source, and the second output's step raises, so `try path(...) catch "c"` answers `"c"` and `del(try (-(...)))` answers the document, as in jq; the resolver binds a call whose body is a comma per leaf of that body, as it does for a comma written at the call site (#4565), and the fresh walk now reads such a body as fresh on its other output. The same source inside another fold's body (`reduce 1 as $i (.; reduce (def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .) as $j (.; .))`, also under a `foreach`'s UPDATE and EXTRACT) raises as well; the value-mode `del(try (-(reduce 1 as $k (.; reduce SRC as $j (.; .)))))` is still answered. Still accepted: a `null` source, a call whose `def` hands the register on next to a fresh value (`def f: ., {a:1}`, also behind a `.` stage) when it is inside another fold's body (routed there, the register leaf refuses as a guess where jq answers a value-mode operand), and a `?//` chain with a bare `$var` alternative inside another fold's body (which alternative it lands on depends on where the enclosing source left jq's register).
