- **jq: a top-level `reduce` source that destructures a fresh value now raises where jq raises for a `?//` chain, a `(def f: ...; f)` bound expression, a variable, a closure argument and a path-expression INIT** (#4522).
  #4494 routed `reduce (def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .)` through the resolver; these spellings of the same destructure still took the by-value drive, which answered where jq exits 5 (`near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":1}`), so `del(...)` of them printed `null` or `{}` (the document deleted) and `... |= 9` replaced it. `def f: {a:1}; f as [$a] ?// {a:$a} | .`, `(def f: {a:1}; f) as {a:$a} | .`, `f as $x | $x as {a:$a} | .`, `def g(x): x; g(def f: {a:1}; f as {a:$a} | .)` and `reduce SRC as $k (.a; .)` (the source raises whatever INIT left the register on) now raise. A chain with a bare `$var` alternative to retry onto, the register behind a variable or a closure argument, and the value-mode spelling are still answered, as in jq. Still accepted: a call whose `def` hands the register on next to a fresh value (`def f: ., {a:1}`), a `reduce` source inside another fold's body, a fresh value piped into a destructure of `.` (`{a:1} | . as {a:$a}`) or after a destructure of the register (`g(.) | g({a:1})`), a parameterised call as the bound expression (`def f(x): {a:1}; f(.) as {a:$a}`, `def g(x): x; g(f) as {a:$a}`), a `//` operand, a `?//` chain with a bare alternative whose body destructures it (`f as $x ?// $y | $y as {a:$a}`), and a `null` source.
