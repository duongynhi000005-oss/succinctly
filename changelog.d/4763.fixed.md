- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` destructure `foreach` that is the UPDATE of a `reduce` is refused under `try ... catch H`, `first(..)` and `limit`/`nth`, as jq refuses it** (#4763, #4754).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (.; try (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)) catch .))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` raises `Invalid path expression with result null` in jq and answered `[[]]` here,
  as did `first(FE)?`, `first(FE)`, `limit(1; FE)` and `nth(0; FE)`, and the `del`/`|=`/`=` that write through them wrote at the root (`= 9` printed `9`). The `foreach` emits nothing and raises nothing, so a handler
  never runs and the wrapper emits nothing either; only `catch empty` and a bare `try`/`?` were read before. A `foreach` source's own `try ... catch H` is unchanged (its handler runs on the raise and emits).
  A `limit` whose count is not a literal, and the `null` state's use by a stage after the `reduce` (`| try .b`, `// .a`), are as they were.
