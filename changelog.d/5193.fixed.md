- **jq: a generator in a `foreach` UPDATE delivers every output** (#5193). `path(foreach .a as $i (5; range(3); $i))` yielded one path where jq yields three, and a bind of an outer `$x` ahead of the generator (`$x as {a:$a} | range(3)`) lost them the same way, so `|=` ran its filter fewer times. The UPDATE was resolved under `path()`'s stop-after-first bound for a leaf; a `foreach` consumes all of its UPDATE's outputs.
