  `path(foreach (1) as $i (.; try (. as {a:$q} | .); $i))` on `{"a":1}` is `Invalid path expression with result 1` in jq (the EXTRACT's value: each UPDATE output is stored as the state and none is path-checked) and named `{"a":1}` here; `null` as EXTRACT on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` and `empty` is no error, where both were refused. The same for `(. as {a:$q} | .)?`, `label $l | (...)`, `(...), .`, `(...), (...)`, `(...) | select(true)`, `select(true) | (...)` and `. as {a:$q} | (., .)`, in `path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`. Over 4,800 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (3 sources x 16 UPDATEs x 5 EXTRACTs x 5 write contexts x 4 documents, against a `main` build): no wrong accept and no row that matched jq stopped matching; 221 rows refused wrongly now answer, and 436 rows that differed only in the error message and 242 more that differed in stdout now match. The shapes left (a bind under an `if`, a `//` or `first(...)`, or beside a comma that holds an `empty` or a non-bind sibling, or behind a `select` that is not `select(true)`) keep their old refusal.
