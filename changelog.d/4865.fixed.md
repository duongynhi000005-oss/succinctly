- **`succinctly yq` writes a single-quoted or plain scalar holding U+2028 or U+2029 the way yq's emitter does** (#4865). yq's scanner reads either character as a line break and writes the scalar single-quoted across lines, the break followed by the indent: `a: 'x\u2028y'` is `a: 'x\u2028  y'` and a flow `a: [x\u2029y]` is `a: ['x\u2029    y']`, where `succinctly yq` echoed the source text. The streaming route and the write (DOM) route both do it, including a flow collection's items and `-I`. U+0085 (which yq also folds to a space on read) and a space beside the break stay open.
