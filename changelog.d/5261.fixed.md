- **yq: an `as` source that raises after an absent key raises again** (#5261). `(.zz | error("x")) as $x | 1` on `a: 1` printed `1`; it now exits 1 with `Error: x` as yq does. The cause was wider than the issue named: yq's pipe hands its right side the zero nodes a missing key leaves behind, and a stage that does not read its context still runs on them, so `(.zz | "s") as $x | $x` binds `"s"` and `(.zz | [.]) as $x | $x` binds `[]` in yq while `(.zz | length) as $x | [$x]` binds nothing. A source that bound nothing now takes the rule an assignment's right side and an `and`/`or`/arithmetic operand already followed, and `error("x")` joins the stages it admits, so `.a = (.x.zz | error("x"))`, `(.x.zz | error("x")) and true` and `1 + (.x.zz | error("x"))` raise too. `error(.q)`, the bare `error`, `|=`, an `as` in a path or write position, and a comma source with one empty branch and one that is not keep their old answer, recorded in `docs/compliance/yq/limitations.md`.
