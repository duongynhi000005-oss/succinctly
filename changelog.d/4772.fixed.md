- **jq: a `foreach` source that is a `?//` chain with a string-literal computed key (`{("b"):$b}`) walks the `null` register as the literal key does** (#4772, #4756).
  `null | path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach ($x as {("b"):$b} ?// $b | .) as $k (.; .; .)) | $x)` prints `["b"]` in jq and printed `[]` here (so did `null | (.|.) as {("a"):$a} ?// $a | .` as the source). A key that cannot raise never retries onto the bare alternative; one that may (`error("q")`) keeps the by-value drive.
