- **jq: a `try ... catch` over a destructuring bind whose body holds a `.`, or a bind behind a navigating stage, no longer hides the register from the stage after it** (#4490).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (try (. as [$a] | .) catch 7) | $x)` and
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (try (.a | . as [$a] | $a) catch 7) | $x)` on `[true]` raised `Invalid path expression with result` where jq answers `[]`,
  and `del`/`=` through such a `$x` refused the same way. A destructuring bind now states against its own entry: a walk that took a step
  hands its body a seed carrying the register it moved, so only a bind that took no step reads as the stage's entry, and a bind's source
  and computed keys (subexpressions) no longer make a pipe opaque. The same bind as a comma sibling (`1, (. as [$a] | .)`) is read too.
  Over 30 new sweep operands (a seeded 40,000-row sample of the full grid, plus their 32,787 `--stage-only` rows) and the 13
  #4129 operands (14,223 `--stage-only` rows): no match lost, no wrong accept added. A pattern with a computed key, a producer in
  the body after a navigating stage (`try (.a | . as [$a] | .) catch 7`) and a fold's UPDATE that destructures with a computed key
  (#4527, already wrong on `main`) are unchanged.
