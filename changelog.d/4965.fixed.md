- **jq: `strptime` fails on text left over after the format and appends what follows white space, as jq does** (#4965). `"2020-01-01x" | strptime("%Y-%m-%d")` answered `[2020,0,1,0,0,0,3,0]`; jq raises `date "2020-01-01x" does not match format "%Y-%m-%d"` (it accepts the result only when the input is consumed up to its end or up to a white-space character), and a leftover that starts with white space (`"2020-01-01 x"`) is appended as a ninth element, `[2020,0,1,0,0,0,3,0," x"]`, which `mktime`, `todate` and `strftime` accept as they do in jq. A literal-only format that leaves input behind (`"abc" | strptime("a")`) is a mismatch too. Formats that consume nothing (`strptime("")`, time-only formats), where jq answers an uninitialised `tm` that is the platform's libc, are unchanged. Over a 1,248-row differential grid against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 (inputs with trailing text, white space and tabs x 13 formats x `mktime`, `todate`, `strftime`, `length`, `.[8]`): 337 differing rows before, 34 after, none that matched differ.
