- **jq: `select($x)` in front of a destructure of `.` now passes the register on by `$x`'s own truthiness in a fold source, as jq does** (#4604).
  `path(.a as $x | reduce .[]? as $i (.; foreach (select($x) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .)))` on `{"a":true}` is `Invalid path expression ...` in jq and printed `[]` here, and `del`, `=` and `|=` through it deleted or replaced the document (also `(. as {a:$a} | .)?` behind the `select`). The fold source's `select(c)` stages are dropped when `c` is the accumulator's own truthiness; a `$x` bound to another node was read only if certified as the accumulator, which a `reduce` body whose source moved the register never does. Any marker holding a truthy value now passes it on. Over `scripts/jq-fold-body-register-grid-sweep.py` (114,660 rows, `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1, against a `main` build) `ACCEPT_WRONG` went 579 to 495 and `MATCH` 109,544 to 109,628, with no lost match; `jq-path-register-sweep.py --sample 20000 --seed 4604` showed no flip. A bare `$x` destructure, `if $x` and `select($x|not)` on a `null` document in a moved body are still accepted (the marker-certification half of #4604).
