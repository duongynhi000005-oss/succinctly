- **yq: `key | tostring` and `key | @format` no longer re-materialize the key node** (#2789).
  `key` emits the key node since #2763, so a one-stage `tostring` or `@format` after it missed the owned
  shortcut and went through the cursor (+49% / +58% on `[.[] | key | tostring]`). A string-decoding value
  now takes the shortcut: `[.[] | key | tostring] | length` over 1.6M keys, 763.9 ms -> 483.7 ms on an
  Apple M4 Pro and 1137.4 ms -> 798.8 ms on an AMD Ryzen 9 7950X; `@json` -22% to -26%, output identical,
  bare `key`, `key | line`, `path` and `.` unchanged.
