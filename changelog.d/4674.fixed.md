- **jq: a `reduce`/`foreach` source `first(first(., X), 2) | . as PATTERN | ..`, or `try first(., X) | ...`, in the body of a `reduce` whose source moved the register, now raises as jq does** (#4674).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (try first(., {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq (`Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":5}`) and printed `null` here; `path(...)` printed `[[]]` and `=`/`|=` replaced the document. #4667 read `first(., X)` as the register `.` only in a source with no `try`/`?` and only as a bare stage. It now also reads a wrapper nested in another (`first(first(., X), 2)`, `limit(1; first(., X), 2)`, `first((., 1), X)`) and a `try`/`?` that wraps the wrapper alone, since the destructure after it sits outside the catch. A `try`/`?` around the destructure still catches the raise, as in jq.
