  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; .); .))` on `null` printed nothing where jq prints `["a","a"]`, `path(reduce .a? as $k (0; -(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; .))))`
  answered `[]` where jq raises `null (null) cannot be negated`, and `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; (reduce (1,2) as $i (1; try .a)); .))` refused where jq prints `["a"]`.
  `fans_out` counted any fold as "maybe splits into siblings" and withheld the register from the UPDATE's frame, so the inner fold's step on a `null` accumulator (#4480) was refused,
  the `try` inside it swallowed the refusal as jq's own, and the step was dropped. A fold is one flow (INIT, source, UPDATE and EXTRACT run in sequence against the frame it is entered
  with), so it no longer counts; a comma, a destructuring bind and a call still do. jq mode only.
