- **jq: a `def` call over a register-keeping body keeps the path register, as in jq** (#4109). `succinctly jq 'path(. as $x | def g: last(.a); g | $x)'` on `{"a":{"b":1},"k":2}` raised `Invalid path expression with result {...}` where jq 1.7.1 prints `[]`, and the same held for a body of `select(f)`, a type filter, a `try` over one, a comma of them, a call of a call and a call inside `first(g)`, through `del`, `=` and `|=`. A zero-argument call is now read as its body is (the resolver already resolves the bound body against the call's own input), within the one shared budget of 16 bodies that #4124's literal-body rule uses. A body that navigates (`def g: .a`, `first(.a)`, `last(.a) | .b`) still moves the register and raises, and a call with an argument stays refused.
