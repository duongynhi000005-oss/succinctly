- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` with a literal `then` beside the caught foreach in its `else` is resolved as that arm behind a `try ... catch` and beside an emitting comma item** (#4800).
  `[path(. as $x | foreach .a as $i (.; try (if .a then 1 else foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) end) catch 1; $i))?]` on `{"a":{"a":null}}` is `[["a"]]` in jq and was `[]`; `path`, `del` and `|= 9` raised `Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}` and now match jq, as does the same `if` beside a comma item that cannot move the register (`(U, 7)`).
  The literal `then` is read on the `foreach`'s last step only, where no later step runs the fold arm on the literal it stored; over a two-element source, and past a falsy `elif` condition, the whole-`if` resolve is kept.
