- **`succinctly yq`'s `contains` compares numbers by tag and text, as yq does** (#4886). `[1.5] | contains([1.50])` is `false` in yq, which pairs two scalars with `recursiveNodeEqual` as array subtraction already does (#4136); `succinctly yq` answered `true` by value. The same holds nested (`[{"a":1.50}] | contains([{"a":1.5}])`) and for a top-level scalar (`1.50 | contains(1.5)`). `succinctly jq` is unchanged: jq compares by value.
