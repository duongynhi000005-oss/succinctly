  the `def` was written in (a `$x` bound around it inside the source included), so the source is placed by the resolver as
  a bare `.` is. Over the 28,443 sweep rows of every `def`-bearing fold operand, wrong accepts go from 546 to 12 and wrong
  answers from 49 to 7, with 56 rows that jq answers now refused, all inside a `reduce` UPDATE and all on the new call
  shapes. A `(.|.)` bind, a later bare `?//` alternative and a `$x` bound outside the `foreach` are still accepted (#4320).
