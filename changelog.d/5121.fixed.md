- **jq: a `null` or boolean emission after a passed bind of the register `$x` in a `foreach` body is accepted at the register the bind moved to** (#5121). With `. as $x`-style binds beside a computed `INIT`, `path(.a as $x | foreach .a as $i ({}; try ($x as {b:$q} | .); null))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a","b"]` in jq, and `(.b as $x | foreach .a as $i (.; ($x as [$q] | .)?; $i)) |= 9` writes `{"a":[9],"b":null}`; both raised `Invalid path expression with result {}`, and the uncaught `with result` message named the accumulator where jq names the EXTRACT's value. #5120 handed the register to the body and #5111 left the body's `.` hand-on unchecked in a three-argument `foreach` UPDATE, which together place these rows; the four rows #5120 pinned as ignored now pass and are un-ignored.
