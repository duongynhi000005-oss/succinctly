- **jq: a refused `$x` the resolver knows is bound elsewhere than the path register is caught as jq's** (#5095). On a document holding equal values at two positions, `[path(. as $r | .a as $x | foreach ($x as {b:$q} | .) as $y (.b; .a?; 1))?]` on `{"a":{"b":1},"b":{"b":1}}` and `[path(.a as $x | .b | "lit" | $x as {b:$q} | .)?]` exited 5 where jq gives `[]`: the frame carrying the register named its position, but `$x`'s bind position was not compared with it, so the equal value was read as possibly the register and its refusal escaped `?`/`try` as a guess. A `reduce` nested in a fold source that mentions `$x` also recorded the register it dropped as lost anywhere, though its frame carried the value; it now records the loss at that value, so `[path(.a as $x | foreach ((reduce ($x as {b:$q} | .) as $w (.; .c))?) as $y (.a; .; .))]` is jq's `[]` too. Over 30,000 rows of the fold-source destructure grids against jq 1.7.1, 26 rows now match and no row that matched changes. Outside those grids, a register spelled through a slice (`.a[0:]`) with `$x` bound at its container now refuses where `main` happened to answer under a `?`/`try`; it is recorded in the limitations page.
