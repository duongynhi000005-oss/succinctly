- **jq: a `reduce` source `first(., X) | . as PATTERN | ..` in the body of a `reduce` whose source moved the register is refused as jq raises** (#4667).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (first(., {a:1}) | . as {a:$a} | .) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq (`Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":5}`) and was `null` here, with `path(...)` printing `[[]]` and `=`/`|=` replacing the document. `first(., X)`, `limit(1; ., X)` and `nth(0; ., X)` emit only their comma's first leaf, so the destructure meets `.`, which the moved body's accumulator is not; the walk read the comma as opaque. The fresh-item twins (`first({a:1}, .)`) and the same source outside a moved body are unchanged.
