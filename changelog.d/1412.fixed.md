- **`succinctly yq` no longer evaluates an assignment's right side when its left side is all no-ops or has no target** (#1412). `5 | (.a, .b) = error("boom")`, `"a" as $k | .[$k] += error("boom")` and `select(false) = error("boom")` raised `boom`; yq answers the document unchanged, because it resolves the left side first and only runs the right side for a target that exists. `+=`/`-=`/`*=` and `=` all follow, and a dynamic left side is now resolved once instead of twice. `succinctly jq` is unchanged: jq runs the right side first.
