- **jq: a later step of a `foreach` UPDATE comma whose state makes an `if` condition raise now emits the items ahead of the `if`** (#5112). With `FE = foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)`, `[path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.a) as $i (.; (7, if .a then FE else 1 end); $i))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[["a"],["a"],["a"]]` in jq: step 2 starts from the `1` the `else` stored, runs `7`, and only then does `.a` of that `1` raise. The comma was resolved whole and refused before `7` emitted, so `?` truncated the output to two paths. Such a comma is now resolved one item at a time, the raising `if` refused as before. Over two 12,000-row grids against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 65 rows that were wrong now match, none that matched are lost, and no wrong accept is new; a 6,000-row control (fold in the `then`) changes 34 rows, all to a match.
