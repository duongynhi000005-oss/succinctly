- **jq: `path`/`|=` no longer write the document root when a `foreach`'s UPDATE has moved the register (`null` documents)** (#4800, #4797).
  `null | (. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; 1 | foreach ($x as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .); $i)) |= 9` is `{"a":9}` in jq (the nested `foreach`'s pattern step on `$x` moves the register to `.a`, where the `null` `$i` is by kind) and was `9` here, with `path(...)` answering `[]`. A computed EXTRACT after an UPDATE that may have moved the register off the root cannot place it, so the terminal's `null` carve-out now declines it: `path`, `del`, `|=` and `limit`/`?` around them refuse with `Invalid path expression with result null` (the resolver's guess, not hidden by a `try`). The bare nested `foreach` and `select(true) | FOREACH` still answer `["a"]`. Not behind a `//`, which backtracks a falsy left side. Over 20,000 differential rows against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1: 0 lost matches, the wrong accepts that remain are the `// 5` shapes.
