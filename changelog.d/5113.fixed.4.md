- **jq: a `foreach` that is the whole UPDATE of an outer `foreach` and whose source is a caught destructure of an outer `$x` emits nothing on a step whose `$x` is an object or array other than the step's register** (#5113). The step's register is the element the outer source moved onto, so the pattern's step fails jq's `path_intact`, the `try` leaves the source empty and the outer step stores `null`. `. as $x | [path(foreach (.a) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); $i))]` on `{"a":null}` is jq's `[]`, and `|= 9` there is jq's `{"a":null}`; it answered `[["a","a"]]` and wrote `{"a":{"a":9}}`, and refused (exit 5) for most other EXTRACTs. Over 120,000 sampled rows (three seeds) of a grid of `$x` binds x outer sources x INIT x UPDATE wrappers x EXTRACT x write contexts x documents against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build, 3,202 rows moved to jq's answer, none became a new wrong answer, no refusal became a wrong answer, and 1 row that matched jq now refuses (an index error on a `null` state that jq raises itself, see `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`). A `null` or boolean `$x`, a `?//` chain and the `$x | . as P` spelling keep their answers.
