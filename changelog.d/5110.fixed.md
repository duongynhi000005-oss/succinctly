- **jq: a literal stage ahead of a nested `foreach` that destructures a `$x` marker, in a `foreach` UPDATE, no longer loses the register the bind moved to** (#5110). `path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; 1 | foreach ($x as {a:$a} | .) as $y (.; .; .); $i))` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq (and `|= 9` writes `{"a":9}`, `del(..)` is `null`): the `1` stage moves nothing, the nested `foreach`'s source steps the register from `$x` to `$x.a`, and EXTRACT's `$i` is judged there. It raised `Invalid path expression with result null`, judging the emission at the root. The bare nested `foreach` has been restated at the register its bind moved to since #4957; a literal stage directly ahead of the nested `foreach` (alone, or behind `try`, a comma, or `(1 | FE) | stage`) is now read through too, where the source cannot move the register and every `$x` the UPDATE reads is the value the fold was entered with (a marker bound elsewhere, such as a `null` from an absent key beside a container register, is not the register and keeps raising). The same literal stage behind a `//` (`(1 | FE) // 5`, a truthy literal head, a caught source, a literal alternate) is read too: `path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; (1 | foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)) // 5; $i))` on `null` is `["a"]`, and `|= 9` writes `{"a":9}` (a `//`-weighted grid of 56,000 rows: 80 now match, none that matched differ). Over 30,000 grid rows (document x bind site x literal x nested `foreach` x wrapper x source x INIT x EXTRACT x context) against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build: 210 rows that were refused or answered wrongly now match, none that matched differ.
