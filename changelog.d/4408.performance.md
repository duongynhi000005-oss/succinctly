- **jq: `.[$i:][-1] | key` and `.[$i:][0] | .a | key` no longer walk the whole array per read** (#4408). #4286 answered `E[a:b][k] | key` from the slice's range, but only for a non-negative `k` inside the slice and a `key` straight after the read; a negative index, an index outside the slice and a field step before the `key` went through the path-context walk, which converts the target array and then the slice per read, so a loop over them was quadratic. The fusion now takes an index of either sign as written (jq keeps it in the path as written, so the key is `k` itself, in or out of the slice) and field steps before the `key` (the key is the last field's name; the one element the steps read is converted, `null` outside the slice, and a step over a scalar still raises through the walk). Over 4,000 elements, `[range(length-1;-1;-1) as $i | .[$i:][-1] | key] | length` takes 22 ms (was 9,085 ms) and `.[$i:][0] | .a | key` 23 ms (was 7,374 ms) on an Apple M4 Pro (release build, one run each, box not idle); the old times quadrupled per doubling of the array and the new ones are flat. Output is identical to the previous build on 7,776 filter/document combinations and to `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1's `path(...) | last` on the 2,592 of them jq answers.
