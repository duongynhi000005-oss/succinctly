- **jq: a `?//` chain, a `first(..)`/`limit(N; ..)` wrapped head and a plain `as $q` bind over a navigation of a `$x` marker onto the register are no longer refused** (#5181). #4827, #5030 and #5180 let a destructure whose source navigates from a `$x` marker pass jq's register check when the node it reaches is the register; four neighbouring spellings reach the same node and were still refused at the step. On `{"a":{"b":1,"c":{"b":3}},"b":2}`, `path(. as $x | .a | $x.a as {b:$q} ?// $q | $q)` and `path(. as $x | .a | $x | first(.a) as {b:$q} | $q)` are `["a","b"]` and `path(. as $x | .a | $x.a as $q | $q)` is `["a"]` in jq, where each raised `Invalid path expression`. A `?//` chain is now read like a single pattern (it passes the first step, and retries onto the next alternative only when the body fails, as before: `path(. as $x | .a | $x | .b as {b:$q} ?// $q | $q)` still raises on `2`), `first(..)` and `limit(N; ..)` with a literal `N >= 1` are walked, and a plain bind of a marker navigation binds the node the walk reached, which shares the marker's storage, so `$q` is the register wherever it shares storage with it. Still refused where jq answers (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`): `$x | .a as $q | $q` (a plain bind over a marker's `.` handed on by a stage) and a `foreach` whose INIT is `$x`.
