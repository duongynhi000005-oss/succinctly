- **jq: a `$x` bound outside and destructured with an `empty` body, as a `foreach` source inside a navigating fold's body, now raises as jq does** (#4626).
  `path(. as $x | foreach .[]? as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as [$a] | empty) catch .) as $y (.; .; .); .))` on `[true]` is `Invalid path expression with result [true]` in jq and printed nothing here, and `del(...)` of it deleted the document; the bare `$x as [$a] | empty` source was answered too.
  The register has moved onto the element while `$x` is still bound to the document, so the pattern's `INDEX` step raises; a `try` whose handler emits then makes the fold emit its accumulator, which is not the register. A handler that emits nothing, a `try` with no handler, a `?` and a `$x` that is the register are still answered, as in jq.
