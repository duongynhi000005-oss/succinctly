- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if` behind a wrapper is resolved as the arm its step ran** (#4800).
  `[path(. as $x | foreach .a as $i (.; try (if .a then foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) else null end); .a))?]` on `{"a":null}` is `[["a","a"]]` in jq and was `[]`: only a bare `if` was resolved as its arm, so a wrapper left the `foreach` in the arm that did not run to withhold the tracking of the `null` a literal `else` stores.
  A comma whose other items emit nothing, a `label` scope without a `break`, and a `try`/`?`/`. |` around a state that took a literal `else` now resolve that arm alone; the same holds under `path`, `del`, `|=`, `try ... catch`, `?` and an `elif`.
