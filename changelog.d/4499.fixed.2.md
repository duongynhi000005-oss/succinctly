- **jq: an `isempty` over a `foreach` in a fold's UPDATE follows the live `null` accumulator** (#4499).
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; isempty(foreach (1,2) as $i (1; try .a; .)); $k))` on `null` is `["a","a"]` in jq and raised `Invalid path expression with result null` here (and `foreach . as $k (...)` answered `[]` for jq's `["a"]`): the operand fold was counted as siblings, which withheld the register from its UPDATE, so the caught `try .a` step was refused as jq's own and dropped. The error for `...; .` as EXTRACT now says `result false`, as jq's does. A nested fold that reads an outer frozen `$var`, an `isempty` over a `reduce`, an inner EXTRACT that reads the loop variable and a non-`null` accumulator are unchanged (still refused where jq answers).
