- **jq: the `any`/`all`/`isempty` verdict stage behind a `def` call or a `reduce` answers as jq does** (#3826). `path(. as $x | def f: any; f | $x)` and `path(. as $x | reduce 1 as $i (.; any) | $x)` are `[]` in jq 1.7.1 and refused with `Invalid path expression with result {...}`; the verdict stage is now read through a zero-arity `def` call into its body, and a `reduce` whose `UPDATE` is a bare `any`/`all` or a verdict over a generator that moves nothing leaves the register at the post-INIT value. An `UPDATE` that iterates the accumulator after a source that moved the register (`reduce .[] as $i (.; any)`), or on a second pass, still raises as in jq. A call with an argument and a verdict over a navigating generator inside a `reduce` stay refused (the safe direction); jq mode only.
