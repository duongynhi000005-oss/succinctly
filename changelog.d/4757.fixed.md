- **jq: a `?//` chain under a `reduce` inside a `foreach` UPDATE now answers on an untracked entry, as jq does** (#4757).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach (1,2) as $j (.; (reduce 1 as $m (.; (.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .)); .)) | $x)` on `[[1],[1]]` is `[] []` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result [[1],[1]]` here,
  and so was the chain as a `foreach` source in the `reduce`'s UPDATE, as the `reduce`'s own source (also behind a call with arguments or a nested `foreach`), and under a handler-less `try`.
  A `reduce` backtracks its source and UPDATE, so the register it leaves is INIT's; the enclosing `foreach` now reads that statement the way it reads a nested `foreach`'s.
  A chain beneath `first`/`limit`/`nth` there (the retry after the consumer's stop emits once more: four paths where jq gives two) and a `reduce` or `foreach` UPDATE inside a `reduce` UPDATE stay refused.
