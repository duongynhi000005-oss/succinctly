- **jq: a caught destructure of a `null` or boolean `$x` that is the whole UPDATE of a `foreach` step is placed where jq's register moves, so the steps it does not pass can be read as empty** (#5205). `.a as $x | [path(foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; (foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (null; .; .)); $i))]` on `{"a":null}` is jq's `[["a","a"]]`: the marker is `jv_identical` to the `null` register of the first step and passes it, moving the register onto the bound child, and fails on the second. The nested `foreach` was read only with an INIT of `.`, so a literal INIT (`null`, `0`, `false`, `true`) left the first step's emission at `["a"]` (and `del`/`|=` writing through it), and the second step was refused (`Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`). `bare_bind_foreach_update_key` now reads a literal INIT, and `caught_destructure_of_other_container` lifts its container-only restriction for a step whose UPDATE has that key. Over 60,000 sampled rows of that shape (`scripts/jq-foreach-update-null-marker-sweep.py`) and 36,000 of the neighbouring #5209 family (`$x` binds x sources x INITs x UPDATE wrappers x EXTRACTs x `path`/`?`/`|=`/`del` x null- and boolean-bearing documents; `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 against a `main` build) 803 rows that were refused or answered wrongly now match and none that matched differs. Lifting the restriction for every UPDATE was rejected: it turned a raise into a write in 8 of 12,000 rows (a nested INIT that navigates).
