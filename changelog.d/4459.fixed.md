- **jq: a `try` or `(...)?` around a `reduce` `UPDATE`'s navigation of the accumulator is now read as navigating, so after a source that moved the register the path error it raises is caught as jq catches it** (#4459).
  `(reduce .[]? as $i (.; try (.a | not) catch 1)) // .a` on `{"a":true}` printed `["a"]` where jq raises on the handler's `1`, and `del(...)` of
  it deleted `.a`; `isempty(try .a)` and `try isempty(.[]?) catch 1` were wrong the same way. The other direction too: `try .a`, `try .[]?`, `(.a)?`
  and `try .a catch false` as the `UPDATE` were refused where jq falls through to the `//` alternative and answers `["a"]`, and `try .a catch "x"`
  now quotes the caught `"x"` as jq does. The fold kept the accumulator at the register and ran the body by value, so the error never happened;
  the body now goes through the live resolver, which catches it, and the handler (which runs only after a raise) is not read. A caught
  navigation among a comma's siblings is not read (`reduce` keeps the last output, so `((.k)?, .)` stays `[]`). Over the 89 `try`/`?`/`isempty`
  fold operands in `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (13 added): wrong accepts 32 -> 10 and wrong refusals 2,291 -> 2,281 on the 79,059
  `--stage-only` rows, 45 -> 28 / 1,054 -> 1,046 and 28 -> 18 / 1,102 -> 1,093 on two 50,000-row seeded samples of the full grid, no stage-only
  regression. The samples show four rows (a fold as the left of an `and`/`or` inside a `//`) that `main` answered by evaluating the fold's value
  by the wrong route and that are now refused, in the safe direction (#4459); the wrong accepts left are `foreach` shapes the same on `main`.
