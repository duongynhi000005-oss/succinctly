- **jq: a bind of an outer `$x` whose body is a comma of `.` delivers every output in a `foreach` UPDATE** (#5185). `path(. as $x | foreach .a as $i (5; $x as {a:$a} | (.,.); $i))` yielded one path where jq yields two (three for `(.,.,.)`), so `|=` ran its filter fewer times. The body was handed on as one branch whenever it only emitted `.`; a comma of `.` now resolves item by item.
