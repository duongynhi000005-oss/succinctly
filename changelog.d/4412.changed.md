- **jq: `path(try .d)` no longer raises on an undecodable string elsewhere in the document** (#4412). `path(try E)` and `path(try E catch empty)` read only the nodes they navigate, as `path(E?)` and `path(.d)` do since #2168. They used to copy the whole input and raise on `{"a":"\ud800","d":5}` where `path(.d?)` answered `["d"]`; the three spellings of one question now agree. Real jq rejects the document at parse in every spelling, the divergence #2168 recorded for `path(.d)`; a body that reads the corrupt value (`path(try .a[])`) still raises. Recorded in `limitations.md`.
