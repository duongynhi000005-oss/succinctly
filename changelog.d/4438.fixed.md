  `path(reduce .[1+1] as $k (.; (reduce .[]? as $i (.; .))))` on `[]` printed `[]` where jq raises `Invalid path expression near attempt to
  iterate through []`, and `del(reduce .[]? as $k (.; (reduce .a? as $i (.; 5))))` on `[true]` printed `null` where jq raises: the inner
  fold's source and INIT run from the outer accumulator and are not subexps, but the outer fold read the nested one as inert and accepted.
  The source is read behind any INIT but a literal `empty` (`first(.)`, `(.)?`, a `def` call all let it run), so
  `(reduce .[]? as $i (empty; .))` keeps its answer. Over the 13 nested-reduce operands in `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (7 added):

  change in wrong refusals in either.
