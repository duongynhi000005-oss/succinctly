- **jq: a caught foreign-`$x` `foreach` beside a comma item that emits nothing, followed by a stage, in a `foreach` UPDATE over a moving source, keeps jq's answer** (#4800).
  `path(. as $x | foreach (.a,.) as $i (.; ((foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .)), empty) | .; .))` on `{"a":null}` is `["a"]` in jq and was refused naming the state (`Invalid path expression with result {"a":null}`); with a `$i` EXTRACT the refusal named `null` where jq names the state, and with `empty` it printed nothing for a refusal jq does not make. The path-mode pipe over a comma carries the stage into each item (`((FE), empty) | .` is `((FE) | .), (empty | .)`), and `empty | .` was not read as an item that emits nothing: it is now, so the comma is the bare `foreach` behind the stage (`path`, `del`, `|=` and `?`, with `.`, `$i`, `empty` and `.a` EXTRACTs, and a `reduce` UPDATE). Over two 12,000-row randomised differential grids against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a build without the change (4 foreach spellings x 5 empties x 15 stages x 11 wrappers x 4 sources x 4 EXTRACTs x 5 documents): 585 rows that were refused or worded differently now match, none that matched differ, and no accepted answer changed.
