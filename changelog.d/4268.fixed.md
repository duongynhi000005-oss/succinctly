- **yq: `.a[0] | head_comment` of a scalar item written `- # c` / `    value` answers `c`** (#4268).
  The comment after the dash is the scalar item's head comment in yq; it was read as nothing. A trailing `- x # t` is still a line comment and a mapping or sequence item still keeps its comment on the dash line. A flow collection item (`- # c` / `  [1]`) answers its dash comment as its head too, and comment lines on their own line between the dash and a scalar or flow value (`-` / `  # f` / `  z`) head that value, where they used to ride on to the next item (`.[1] | head_comment`) and print after the item. Still different: a blank line between the comment and the value, and a dash comment together with an own-line one (the order of the two).
