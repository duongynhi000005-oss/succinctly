- **yq: `column` counts characters, not bytes** (#4364). A multi-byte character earlier on the line shifted the column: `é: [1]` reported
  `[1,5]` where yq says `[1,4]`, and `"日本": !!str 5` `[1,11]` against yq's `[1,7]`. A character is a Unicode scalar value, as go-yaml counts
  runes (`e` plus a combining accent is two columns, an astral character one). An ASCII document is answered from the byte column with no
  scan of the line; `yq-locate`, `to_offset` and `YamlIndex::to_line_column` stay in bytes. `line` is unchanged.
