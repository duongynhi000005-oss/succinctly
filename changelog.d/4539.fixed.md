- **jq: a `foreach`/`reduce` whose UPDATE is a `try` over a computed-key `?//` bind no longer loses its element** (#4539).
  `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; try (. as {(.):$a} ?// $a | $a); $k))` is `["a"]` in jq and was nothing here, and `path(foreach .a? as $k (0; try (. as {(.):$a} ?// $a | $a); .))`,
  which jq raises, answered with no output (`reduce` answered `[]`). The walk's first step (`0 | .[0]`, the accumulator as the key) can never succeed, which jq raises
  whatever its register is and `?//` retries onto the bare alternative; `pattern_walk_fails_by_value` cannot judge a computed key, so the refusal read as a guess, was
  never retried, and the plain error it returned was swallowed by the `try`. The step's own probe (`refused_step_could_succeed`, the one the folds read since #3998) now
  settles it. Not just the accumulator as the key: any computed key whose step cannot succeed (`{(.|tostring):$a}` over a number). Over all 227 `?//` operands
  (229,166 `--stage-only` rows and a 60,000-row seeded full-grid sample): no match lost, `ACCEPT_WRONG` 206 -> 136. A container accumulator
  (`[]`, `{}`) under the same shape still answers where jq raises (#4550).
