- **jq: a `foreach` source that destructures `limit(1; ., ..)` is refused where jq raises, as `first(., ..)` is** (#4514).
  `limit(1; ., 1)` (a count of at most one) is its comma's first leaf, the register, so `path(foreach (limit(1; ., 1) as {a:$a} | .) as $k (.; .; .))`
  on `{}` printed `[]` and `del`/`=` wrote, where jq raises `Invalid path expression`; on `null` jq prints `["a"]`. The same
  through a nested comma (`limit(1; (., 1), 2)`, also `first((., 1), 2)`), a fractional count (`limit(0.5; ..)`) and a frozen
  `$x` (`limit(1; $x, 1)`). Over the 17 new operands in `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (17,629 `--stage-only` rows) against
  `main`: wrong accepts 590 -> 14, DIFF 47 -> 7, matches 16,768 -> 17,252; the 132 rows that stop matching are refusals in
  contexts where the operand's `.` is not the register (`first(., 1)` refuses there too), the safe direction. A seeded
  30,027-row sample of the whole grid: wrong accepts 39 -> 24, the 4 lost matches are the same refusals. Still open: `limit(2; ., 1)`,
  a comma as a call argument, and a `reduce (label $o | .)` source followed by a destructure.
