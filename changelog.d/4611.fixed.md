- **jq: a `foreach` with a navigating source and an `isempty`/`any`/`all` UPDATE keeps the register for EXTRACT** (#4611).
  `path(foreach .[]? as $k (0; isempty(1); $k))` on `[1]` is `[0]` in jq and was `Invalid path expression with result 1` here, and so were `isempty(empty)`, `(1 | isempty(.))`, `any(1; .)`,
  `all(1; .)` and the same shapes with INIT `.`, and the `del(...)`/`=` twins. jq runs the verdict builtin's generator and backtracks, so the register is where UPDATE entered (the element the
  source navigated to) whenever neither operand can move it, and EXTRACT's `$k` IS that register. The fold treated any builtin-call UPDATE as one that lost the register; the verdict builtins
  over operands that cannot move it are now read as leaving it in place. An operand that navigates (`isempty(.a?)`, `any(.[]?; .)`), the bare `any`/`all` (which iterate their input) and a
  computed boolean as EXTRACT are still refused, as in jq.
