- **jq: a navigation after `first(.)`, `limit(1; .)`, `nth(0; .)` or `first(., X)`, as a `reduce` source in a moved `reduce` body, now raises as jq does** (#4685).
  `del(reduce .[]? as $i (.; reduce (first(.) | .a) as $j (.; .)))` on `{"a":5}` exits 5 in jq (`Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "a" of {"a":5}`) and printed `null` here, so `del` deleted the document and `=`/`|=` replaced it; `path(...)` of it printed `[[]]`. `.[]?`, `..` and `select(.) | .a` after the wrapper were accepted too.
  The nested fold's source was read only up to the first stage that was not `.`, a marker or a `select`, so the navigation behind the wrapper was never seen and the step stayed trackable. A builtin that reads by value (`getpath`, `keys`), a fresh first leaf (`first({a:1}, .)`) and a `try` around the navigation are unchanged, as in jq.
