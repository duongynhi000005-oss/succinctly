- **yq: `map(.)` over an alias of a non-empty container detaches, and over a merge-only mapping keeps its position** (#4690).
  `printf 'a: &x [1]\nb: *x\n' | yq '.b | map(.) | path'` is `[]` in yq and printed `["b"]` here (an alias node has a position of its own and no children), and `a: {<<: {}}` with `.a | map(.) | path` is `["a"]` in yq and printed `[]` (the cursor saw the `<<` entry as a child). The cursor site of `map`'s identity rule now asks the node what it presents (an alias's target, a mapping's merged members) instead of whether it has a subtree, as the owned site already did by value; `key` and `parent` after `map(.)` follow.
