- **jq: a slice past offset 65,535 of a value that is itself a view of a longer array is refused, as jq refuses it** (#5118). jq's slice is a view of the array's buffer with a 16-bit offset and copies from offset 65,536, so two evaluations of such a slice are two arrays; the resolver records each node below the root at offset 0 and read `[range(70003)] | .[60000:] | del(.[6000:] as $x | .[6000:] | $x)` as one node, deleting through it where jq exits 5 (the same below a field, after `.[0] = 0`, and after `+ []`). Once a slice has been resolved against a container of more than 65,536 elements on the thread, every slice that starts past 0 is read as possibly copied. That is loud: it also refuses two rows jq answers over such an array (`[range(70003)] | del(.[6000:] as $x | .[6000:] | $x)`), recorded in `docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`; arrays of 65,536 elements or fewer are unaffected.
