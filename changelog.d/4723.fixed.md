- **jq: a `foreach` whose UPDATE is a `foreach` over a caught foreign-`$x` destructure emits nothing, as jq does** (#4723, residual of #4709). `path(.a as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); .))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` printed `[]` once per outer step where jq 1.7.1 prints nothing and exits 0 (`del`, `|=` and `=` hand the document back in jq; here they wrote through the phantom path): `$x` is not the register, so the pattern's path-checked step raises, the `try` catches it, the inner `foreach` is empty, so the UPDATE is empty and the outer `foreach` has nothing to emit. #4722 marked the bare-`foreach` UPDATE of a `reduce` only; `resolve_foreach` now marks its UPDATE the same way, and the inner fold's source is read as exhausted (on the later steps too, whose `null` state is no register: the `foreign_in_off_body` route) rather than refused, since a `foreach` parent holds no state. The `?//` chain, array patterns and scalar markers behave alike; a handler that emits (`catch .`), a `reduce` inner and a `null` document stay answered.
