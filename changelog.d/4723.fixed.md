- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE that is an inner `foreach` over a caught foreign-`$x` destructure emits nothing** (#4723, #4709).
  `path(.a as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); .))` on `{"a":{"b":1}}` prints nothing in jq and printed `[]` twice here (one path per outer step), with the `[...?]`, `del(...)` and `=` twins, and on `null` (`["a"]` twice in jq) and `{"a":[true]}`. The bind of a `$x` that is not the register raises, the `try` catches it, and the inner source is empty, so the outer `foreach` has no UPDATE output to emit. Over a `reduce` outer the empty source still leaves a `null` state (#4709). The `fe-lit` grid slice drops from 178 to 160 `DIFF` rows with no lost match and no new `ACCEPT_WRONG`.
