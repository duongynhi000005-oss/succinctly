- **jq: a `foreach` over `first(CHAIN)` or `limit(n; CHAIN)`, with `CHAIN` a `?//` chain that has a bare `$var` alternative over the register, now emits its element once in path mode, as jq does** (#4483).
  `path(foreach (first(. as {a:$a} ?// $a | .)) as $k (.; .; .))` on `{"a":true}` printed `[]` twice where jq prints it once (`limit(1; ...)`
  likewise). jq's `first` is a `label`/`break`, which the chain's `?//` catches as a body error, so value mode really does emit twice
  (`[foreach (first(CHAIN)) as $k (.; .; .)]` is two elements in both tools) and the by-value drive reproduced that in path mode too; the chain
  directly under the wrapper is now read as the register's destructure and placed by the resolver, which answers once. Only outside every fold
  body (inside a `reduce`/`foreach` body the routed chain refuses rows jq answers) and only with the wrapper as the chain's direct parent: a
  `try`/`?` around `first(CHAIN)` is still driven by value, since routing it accepted a `del` jq refuses. On the 16,899 `--stage-only` rows over
  the 20 `first`/`limit` chain operands in `scripts/jq-path-register-sweep.py` (17 added): 0 lost matches, wrong accepts 8 -> 0, differing
  answers 125 -> 61.
