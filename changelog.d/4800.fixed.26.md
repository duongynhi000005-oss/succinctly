- **jq: a `foreach` UPDATE `if . then FE else .a end` (an `else` that navigates or computes) is read on the steps that run its `then` branch** (#4800).
  `[path(. as $x | foreach . as $i (.; if . then foreach (try ($x as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .) else .a end; $i))?]` on `{"a":true}` is `[]` in jq and `del(...)` of the same UPDATE with an `empty` EXTRACT echoes the document; both were refused naming the state, uncatchably under `?`, because the static read of the whole `if` refuses a navigating `else`. A step whose condition is truthy never runs the `else`, so the step is now read as the caught `foreach` alone, as the bare `if . then FE else . end` already was; `else .a`, `else 3`, `else []`, `else error("x")`, `else $x` and `else .a.a`, behind `| .`, `try`/`?` or beside `empty`, answer as jq does (an `else null`, and `else 3` over a moving source on `null`, still differ). Against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1, the 21,875-row grid (5 `if` spellings x 5 `else` arms x 5 sources x 5 EXTRACTs x 5 write contexts x 7 documents) goes from 20,261 to 21,818 matching rows (1,557 fixed, none lost, no wrong accept), and a 12,000-row random control grid over 9 conditions x 14 `else` arms x 5 wrappers x 6 sources reads 767 fixed, none lost, no new wrong accept. The `else null` arm and `else 3` over a moving source on `null` are recorded in `limitations.md`.
