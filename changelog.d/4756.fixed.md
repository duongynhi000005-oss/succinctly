- **jq: a `foreach` source that is a `?//` chain over `null |`, `$x |` or `$x as PAT` on a `null` document walks the register, as jq does** (#4756, #4727).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach (null | (.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .)) | $x)` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq and printed `[]` here, so `=`/`|=`/`del` wrote at the root. Each head is `jv_identical` to a `null` register by kind, so the chain's first alternative moves it onto the matched member. A `.`-headed chain behind a non-register stage is unchanged.
