- **jq: a `foreach` whose source is a `?//` chain behind a `null` or a `$x` bound to `null` walks the `null` register on a `null` document, as jq does** (#4756, #4727).
  `path(. as $x | 1 | (foreach (null | (.|.) as {a:$a} ?// $a | .) as $k (.; .; .)) | $x)` on `null` is `["a"]` in jq and was `[]` here, and so were the same source headed by `$x`
  (`$x | CHAIN`, `$x as {b:$b} ?// $b | .`), at the top level (`path(foreach (null | CHAIN) as $k (.; .; .))`), inside another `foreach`'s UPDATE,
  and in the `=`, `|=` and `del` that write through the path (`= 5` wrote the document `5` where jq writes `{"a":5}`). Every `null` is `jv_identical` to a `null` register by kind,
  so the chain's first alternative walks the register and its `INDEX` steps move it; the by-value drive reported the bare alternative's unmoved register instead.
  The spine walk that routes a `foreach` source to the resolver now reads a `null` stage, a `null` marker and a comma of those as the register, and a `null` marker beside a live `null` register
  no longer withholds it. A stage that is not `null` (`1 |`, `false |`, a navigation in front), a body that navigates, and a `reduce`, which backtracks its source, are as they were;
  on a document that is not `null` a `$x` chain on an untracked entry stays refused where jq answers `[]`.
