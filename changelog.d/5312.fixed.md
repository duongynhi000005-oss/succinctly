- **jq: `fromjson` and `tonumber` skip a leading UTF-8 byte-order mark, as jq does** (#5312). `"\ufeff[1]" | fromjson` raised `Invalid numeric literal` and `"\ufeff1" | tonumber` raised `cannot be parsed as a number`; jq's parser skips one BOM at the front of the text, so both answer `[1]` and `1`. A second BOM, or one behind whitespace, is still refused, and the error's column now counts what follows the first BOM (and a BOM alone reads `Expected JSON value`), as jq's does. yq mode is unchanged.
