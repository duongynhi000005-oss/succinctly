- **jq: a `//` over a bind of an outer `$x` in a `reduce` or `foreach` UPDATE is read when its right operand wraps binds in a comma, an `if` or a generator source** (#5175). #5190 read a right operand that is a bare chain of binds; one with a comma (`// ((.a as $q | $q), 3)`), an `if` (`// (if true then (.a as $q | $q) else 1 end)`) or a bind over a comma or `.[]` (`// ((1,2) as $q | $q)`) kept the resolver's refusal, which a `try`/`?` around the `//` swallowed, so `path`/`del`/`|=` answered (or wrote) where jq raises at the emission of the left operand's truthy output: `path(. as $x | foreach (1,2) as $i (false; try (($x as {a:$a} | 5) // ((.a as $q | $q), 3))))` on `{"a":null}` and `(. as $x | reduce (1,2) as $i (null; try (($x as {a:$a} | 5) // ((.a as $q | $q), 3)))) |= 9` on `null` are `Invalid path expression with result 5` (exit 5) in jq, and answered nothing and `9` here. Every item of such an operand runs from the register the `//` was entered with, so it is read like a bare chain, where each bind body emits at most once and no generator-source bind holds another. Over the sampled grid rows with such an operand (`scripts/jq-fold-update-alt-bind-sweep.py`, against `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 and a `main` build) 43 of 24,873 `reduce`, 209 of 24,873 two-argument `foreach` and 782 of 99,490 three-argument `foreach` wrong accepts now match jq (31, 0 and 4 more raise with another wording of the step), and no row that matched before differs. Still open (`docs/compliance/jq/limitations.md`): a `?//` chain behind the `//` in a `reduce`, a `reduce` whose final accumulator is the register's node, and an operand with a `def` or a call.
