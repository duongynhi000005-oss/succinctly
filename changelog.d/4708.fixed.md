- **jq: a destructure inside a fold that is the source of another fold is path-checked** (#4708). `foreach (foreach (1,2) as $k (.; {a:1} as $n | $n as {b:$b} | .; .)) as $z (.; .; .)` (and the same with `. as {a:$a} | .`, `null as $n | $n as {b:$b} | .`, or a `$x` bound to the document) printed `[] []` from `path()` where jq 1.7.1 raises `Invalid path expression near attempt to access element "b" of {"a":1}`, and `del`/`=`/`|=` wrote through the answer: the outer fold's source was driven by value whenever its own spine held no navigation or destructure, so the bind in the nested fold's body ran unchecked. It reproduces at the root, not only three folds deep. Such a source now goes to the resolver when the nested fold certainly destructures a freshly built value, or, for a `foreach` source, the register; on the `--deep` grid the wrong accepts of the nested-source shapes drop from 4,101 to 236 (`foreach` outer) and from 1,617 to 236 (`reduce` outer).
