- **jq: the `try ($x | . as P | .)` spelling of a caught foreign-`$x` destructure in a sole nested `foreach` UPDATE is read as the bare `try ($x as P | .)` is** (#5206). #5189 (#5113) emptied a nested `foreach` whose source is a caught destructure of an object or array `$x` that differs from the step's register, and read the pipe spelling no further than #5017 had, so `. as $x | [path(foreach (.a) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x | . as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); $i))]` on `{"a":null}` was exit 5 (`Invalid path expression with result null`) where jq answers `[]`, and the same filter under `|= 9` or `del` took the `try` for a refusal. `bare_bind_source_key` reads the pipe spelling (behind a `try` or `?`, for a container `$x`: a `null` or boolean one is #5205's) and `caught_destructure_of_other_container` no longer requires the bare form, so a step the bind passes is placed as it is for the bare spelling and a step it does not is empty. Over a 40,000-row sample (pipe and bare spellings x sources x INITs x UPDATE wrappers x EXTRACTs x `path`/`?`/`|=`/`del` x `. as $x` and `.a as $x`; `/usr/bin/jq` 1.7.1 against a `main` build) 4,622 rows that were refused or answered wrongly now match and none that matched differs; a 30,000-row sample of the neighbouring spellings (a body other than `.`, a stage after it, `// empty`, `first(...)`, an `error` body) fixes 1,120 and moves none the wrong way, and 16,000 rows of the `fam`/`gen3` differential grids regressed nothing. Earlier versions were rejected on those grids: reading a `null` marker regressed 16 rows (it inherits #5205), reading the uncaught spelling 200 (no raise is modelled for it, #5182 gives one to the bare spelling only), and a verdict wider than the key's reading (a body other than `.`, a `// empty` outside) 60. The `?//` chain stays unread.
