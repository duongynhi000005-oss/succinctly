- **jq: the `try ($x | . as P | .)` spelling of a caught foreign-`$x` destructure in a sole nested `foreach` UPDATE is read as the bare `try ($x as P | .)` is** (#5206). #5189 (#5113) emptied a nested `foreach` whose source is a caught destructure of an object or array `$x` that differs from the step's register, and read the pipe spelling no further than #5017 had, so `. as $x | [path(foreach (.a) as $i (.; foreach (try ($x | . as {a:$a} | .)) as $y (.; .; .); $i))]` on `{"a":null}` was exit 5 (`Invalid path expression with result null`) where jq answers `[]`, and the same filter under `|= 9` or `del` took the `try` for a refusal. `bare_bind_source_key` reads the pipe spelling (behind a `try` or `?`, for a container `$x`: a `null` or boolean one is #5205's) and `caught_destructure_of_other_container` no longer requires the bare form, so a step the bind passes is placed as it is for the bare spelling and a step it does not is empty.
