- **yq: a keyed lookup into a wide YAML mapping is one hash probe, not a walk of every key** (#4003). JSON documents got a per-object key index in #3913 (350x at 10,000 keys); a YAML mapping kept the walk, so `[keys[] as $k | .[$k] | .a]` over a 32,000-member mapping took 36 s and over 128,000 members more than 100 s. The same index now serves a YAML mapping once it has been read by key twice and walked at least 64 members, when every key is a plain string: an alias key (yq never matches one by name), a collection key (matched to `""` alone) and a mapping with a `<<` merge key (whose entries no member spells) keep the walk, and the index answers as the walk does, the last duplicate winning. Measured (M4 Pro, release, output identical): that query is 0.75 s at 32,000 members, 0.17 s at 64,000 and 0.21 s at 128,000; `. as $l | [keys[] | $l[.].b]` 0.11 / 0.17 / 0.27 s against 38 s / >100 s. A lookup read once, or a mapping under 64 members, costs what it did. `has` of a YAML mapping still walks.
