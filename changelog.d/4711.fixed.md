- **yq: `style`, `anchor` and the comments of a kept value come from the node it stands at** (#4711).
  The sequel to #4696, which did `line` and `column`: `.a | map(.) | style` on `a: []` answered
  `""` where yq answers `flow`, and `.a | del(.b) | line_comment` lost the comment `.a` carries,
  for every stage that hands yq's node on (a write, `del`, `sort`, `flatten`, `map_values`,
  `pick`, `. + {}`, ...). Each getter has its own list of stages: `unique`, `unique_by` and
  `reverse` keep the style and the line comment but not the line, `to_entries` keeps the line
  comment. Two resets yq performs are modelled: a write that fills an empty flow container makes
  it block, and the merge `*`/`*=` drops the anchor. Captured from yq v4.53.3 over a mapping, a
  sequence, empty containers, a scalar and an alias; swept 7 getters x 36 stages x 8 documents (2016 rows),
  0 rows regressed. `succinctly jq` is unchanged.
